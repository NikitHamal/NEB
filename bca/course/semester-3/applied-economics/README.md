# Applied Economics — BCA 206 (Semester III, 2 credits)

In the revised curriculum BCA 206 is a **2-credit** subject; the syllabus text found is the TU **CAEC353** "Applied Economics" (3 credits, 7 units, 45 hours, also used in the 2074 curriculum's later semester). The unit list below follows that text; if your college's 2-credit syllabus drops a unit or shortens a topic, study the units it names.

| Unit | Title | Hours |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction | 6 |
| 2 | Elasticity of Demand and Supply | 6 |
| 3 | Theory of Consumer Behavior | 6 |
| 4 | Cost and Revenue Curves | 6 |
| 5 | Market Structure | 9 |
| 6 | National Income Accounting | 6 |

## Verification

`bca/tools/check_economics.py` recomputes every numerical example (market equilibrium and tax incidence, all elasticities, the cardinal equilibrium bundle, the Hicksian decomposition, the cost schedule, revenue and MR, monopoly, price discrimination, cartel and cheating profits, national-income aggregates and deflator, multipliers, money multiplier, CPI and BoP arithmetic) and prints the values quoted in the text. The syllabus asks for numerical exercises "using Excel"; Excel formulas are given in Units 2, 4 and 6.

## Source and honesty

Units, topics and hours: TU Applied Economics syllabus as reproduced on benchpartner.com (see `bca/SOURCES.md`); the revised BCA 206 PDF was not retrievable. Statements about Nepal (NRB established 1956/2013 BS, peg to the Indian rupee since 1993, remittance dependence, landlocked transit via India) are well-known facts given without statistics, because current figures change every year; check the latest NRB and Ministry of Finance publications for numbers. Graphs are described in words; draw them from the tables.

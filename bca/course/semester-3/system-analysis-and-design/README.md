## Verification

This is a prose subject, so there is no program output. The **numbers** in the worked examples were recomputed in Python: the critical path (11 weeks, A→C→E→F, slack 1 on B and D), PERT expected time, the weighted-scoring totals, NPV (+447,697), ROI (150%), simple payback (2.0 years), discounted payback (2.35 years), benefit–cost ratio (1.51) and IRR (about 41%), and the decision-table coverage (4 + 2 + 1 + 1 = 8 rules). Diagrams are described in words and tables rather than drawn.

## Source and honesty

Unit titles, topics and hours are the TU **CACS203** syllabus as reproduced on ishan12.com.np (see `bca/SOURCES.md`); the revised BCA 204 PDF was not retrievable. The syllabus follows the textbook *Essentials of Systems Analysis and Design* (Valacich, George, Hoffer), so the order of topics here follows that book's chapters, but all text is original. DFD, ERD and UML diagrams are described, not drawn; draw them yourself with the examples as exercises.

## Verification

Bandwidth, capacity, RAID, PSU/UPS, ppi, energy and MBR-limit figures were recomputed with a script before publishing. The procedures follow vendor manuals and the CompTIA A+ troubleshooting method; nothing here has been tested on a bench by a TU technician.

## Source and honesty

**The unit structure is reconstructed.** The BCA 106 syllabus PDF could not be retrieved; secondary sources only name the subject as a 1-credit practical course on PC hardware. The book covers what such a course must (components, assembly, OS installation, troubleshooting, cabling). Hours per unit are not stated. Always follow your lab's own safety rules and the component manuals; beep codes and header layouts differ between manufacturers.

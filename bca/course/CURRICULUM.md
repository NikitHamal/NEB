# The TU BCA curriculum, semester by semester

**Applies to:** the revised curriculum for students admitted from the **2082 batch**. Students of the 2081 batch and earlier follow the 2074 curriculum (126 credit hours); the code-by-code map is in the second half of this file.

**Honesty note about the source.** The semester and credit lists below were cross-checked on two independently written pages (texasintl.edu.np, September 2026; davcollege.edu.np, July 2026), and they agree with each other and with the TU old-syllabus listings on bcanepaltu.com, nplupdates.com and insightsnp.com. I could not retrieve the revised TU curriculum PDF itself. Per-subject *unit* lists in this repository are taken from the TU syllabus PDFs that were reachable (see `../SOURCES.md`); where a unit list is inherited from the older code of the same subject, the subject README says so. Check your college for the exact notice of your year.

Assessment (both curricula): 60 % TU final examination, 40 % internal (assignments, lab, attendance). Practical work, projects and the internship are examined separately.

## Verification

Prose subject; every number in the worked examples was recomputed in Python in the session: the critical path (A-B-D-F-G = 18 weeks, float 4 on C and E), PERT expected times and probabilities (86.3%, 29.2%, 95% date 15 weeks), crashing costs (Rs 24,000 for 3 weeks), earned value indices (CPI 0.89, SPI 0.83, EAC Rs 560,000), function points (UFP 175, FP 187.25), basic and intermediate COCOMO for 32 KLOC (91.3, 145.5, 230.4 person-months; 140.0 with EAF 1.15), NPV (+18,387 at 10%), IRR (about 18%), payback (2.6 years), risk exposure and RRL, DPMO (5,667), availability (99.01%) and DRE.

## Source and honesty

Unit titles, topics and hours follow the TU **CACS407 Software Project Management** syllabus as reproduced on bcatu.com and notebahadur.com (seven units); the revised BCA 402 text was not retrievable, and the topic list is the same. The treatment follows Hughes and Cotterell, *Software Project Management* (the prescribed textbook family), Boehm's COCOMO, and the PMBOK Guide for terms. The laboratory guide at the end of Unit 7 follows the syllabus' list of tools (MS Project, OpenProj, Trello, Asana, ClickUp).

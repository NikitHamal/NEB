# UX/UI Design — BCA 155 (Semester II, 3 credits)

How to design interfaces people can actually use: research, interaction and visual design, components, wireframes and prototypes, evaluation, and voice and wearable interfaces.

| Unit | Title | Hours (as published) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Introduction to UX and UI | 4 |
| 2 | User Interaction Design and UX Research | 4 |

| 5 | UI Design Considerations | 6 |
| 6 | Wireframing and Prototyping | not stated |
| 7 | Design Evaluation | 6 |
| 8 | Advanced Techniques: VUI and NLP-based UI | not stated |

The syllabus also has a 48-hour lab (design tool, layout, colour and typography, grids, interactive elements, windows and controls, navigation, wireframes and prototypes, storyboards, animation); Unit 6 section 6.6 gives the step-by-step method.

## Verification

`bca/tools/check_uxui.py` recomputes every number in the book: WCAG contrast ratios of the colours quoted, Fitts's and Hick's law examples, SUS scoring, the Nielsen–Landauer sample-size curve, KLM task times, type scales, grid arithmetic and task metrics. This is a design theory subject, so there is no program to compile; the checker covers the numbers only. The Figma lab exercises are described, not executed.

## Source and honesty

The unit list and the published hours (4, 4, 6, 12, 6, 6) come from two web pages that reproduce the TU BCA 155 syllabus (nneducation.com and hamronotes.com). They agree on the eight units; the first lists hours only for six of them, and the second gives all eight units' topics. The official TU PDF was not retrieved. Standard references used for the content: Nielsen's ten heuristics and usability research (NN/g), WCAG 2.x, Preece, Rogers and Sharp *Interaction Design* (DECIDE), Shneiderman's golden rules, Norman's *Design of Everyday Things*, Galitz's *Essential Guide to User Interface Design* (menus, windows and controls, which the syllabus wording follows).

# Class 12 Chemistry lesson scripts

Shared drawing helpers (glassware, atoms, tables, titration curves, indicator colours) live in `_common.py`.
Minutes are narration length from prefetch.py (estimates from word count are marked est.). Each pushed unit has
been checked part by part with frames.py.

| Unit | Title | Narration (est.) | Parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Volumetric Analysis | 30.4 min | cold open, 7 parts (gravimetric/volumetric/equivalent, E and valency, E of compounds, concentration units, standards, law of equivalence, titration), recap |
| 2 | Ionic Equilibrium | 31.4 min | cold open, 15 parts (Arrhenius → hydrolysis), recap |
| 3 | Chemical Kinetics | 22.8 min | cold open, 10 parts (introduction → catalysis), recap |
| 4 | Thermodynamics | 20.8 min | cold open, 10 parts (energy in reactions → ΔG and K), recap |
| 5 | Electrochemistry | 17.7 min | cold open, 7 parts (electrode potential → batteries and fuel cells), recap |
| 6 | Transition Metals | 17.0 min | cold open, 7 parts (characteristics, oxidation states, complex ions, shapes, crystal field theory, colour, catalysis), recap |
| 7 | Studies of Heavy Metals | 27.2 min | cold open, 5 parts (copper, zinc, mercury, iron, silver), recap |
| 8 | Haloalkanes | 17.6 min | cold open, 8 parts (naming and classes, preparation, physical properties, SN1/SN2, eight substitution products, elimination/reduction/Wurtz, making chloroform, reactions of chloroform), recap |
| 9 | Haloarenes | 15.0 min | cold open, 7 parts (naming and isomerism, preparing chlorobenzene, physical properties, low reactivity, reduction and electrophilic substitution, Fittig/Wurtz–Fittig/chloral, uses), recap |
| 10 | Alcohols | 18.0 min | cold open, 7 parts (naming and classes, Victor Meyer and Lucas tests, preparation, industrial preparation, common terms, physical properties, chemical properties with E1 mechanism and a growing reaction map), recap |
| 11 | Phenols | 15.0 min | cold open, 8 parts (naming, preparation, physical properties, acidity with phenoxide resonance and a pKa ladder, NH3/Zn/Na/coupling/phthalic anhydride, acylation/Kolbe/Reimer–Tiemann, electrophilic substitution, tests and uses), growing reaction map, recap |
| 12 | Ethers | 19.5 min | cold open (Morton 1846; the 83 °C boiling-point puzzle), 5 parts (naming, metamerism and isomers; Williamson SN2 with moving mechanism, energy profile, E2 pitfall; physical properties and H-bonding; HI cleavage mechanism, anisole, HCl/H₂SO₄, peroxides, Cl₂; uses and ether extraction), growing reaction map, recap |

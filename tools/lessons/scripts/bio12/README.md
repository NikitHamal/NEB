# Class 12 Biology lesson scripts

Shared drawing helpers live in `_bio.py`: one colour per tissue (xylem sky, phloem pink, cambium amber, cork brown,
sclerenchyma salmon, collenchyma lime, parenchyma mint), tissue patches (`parenchyma`, `collenchyma` angular/lamellar/lacunar,
`sclerenchyma`, `meristem`, `fibre`, `sclereid`), conducting elements (`vessel` annular/spiral/scalariform/reticulate/pitted,
`tracheid`, `sieve_tube`), transverse sections built from real cell geometry (`RootTS`, `DicotStemTS`, `MonocotStemTS`,
`DicotLeafTS`, `MonocotLeafTS`, `mono_bundle`), `Flow` (dots carried along a path), and `Captions` — a single caption slot
where each new caption replaces the last, so running commentary never piles up. `_phys2.py` adds physiology helpers
(guard cells, bending shoots, Mimosa, tally board). Minutes are narration length from prefetch.py. Each pushed unit has been
checked part by part with frames.py.

| Unit | Title | Narration | Parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Plant Anatomy | 33.2 min | cold open (a sal and a bamboo over forty years: why does only one make wood?), 6 parts (Chlamydomonas vs division of labour, the two bases of classification, Sachs' three tissue systems on a stem; meristem cells, origin/position/function (a mown lawn regrowing from intercalary meristems, Haberlandt's zones), de-/re-differentiation, parenchyma → chlorenchyma → aerenchyma, collenchyma bending and its three types, fibres (allo) and sclereids, xylem and phloem elements with flowing water and food, a stretching protoxylem, exarch vs endarch; a root built layer by layer, the Casparian strip forcing water through the living cell, a lateral root breaking out of the pericycle, tetrarch → polyarch; dicot and maize stems, bundle close-ups, bundle-type icons, reading an unknown slide; dorsiventral leaf with CO₂ diffusing in, isobilateral leaf rolling as bulliform cells lose water; secondary growth: the cambial ring closing, one radial file of cells dividing, years of rings centred on stage, spring/autumn wood cell by cell, a stump counted ring by ring, heartwood and tyloses, cork cambium, periderm and bark, a lenticel breathing, events sorted into order, the bamboo answered, the wavy root cambium), 1 worked example per hard idea, 7 quizzes, recap |

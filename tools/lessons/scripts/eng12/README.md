# Class 12 English lesson scripts

Shared helpers live in `_en.py`, which reuses the Mathematics/Biology helpers (`head`, `bullets`, `reveal_list`, `table`,
`reveal_table`, `neby_in/out`) and adds literature props: `quote` (a short quotation card), `card` (titled text card),
`timeline`, `house` and `fence`, `chip`, `arc_curve` (an emotion/plot curve on axes), `device_slide` (device | example |
effect table) and `transform_strip` (before → after for the grammar units). The literature lessons summarise and analyse
each text — plot, characters, themes, structure, devices, exam tips — and quote only short lines; they never reproduce the
text. Minutes are narration length; `python3 tools/lessons/ttskeys.py <script>` lists the narration cache files a unit
uses. Each pushed unit has been checked part by part with frames.py.

| Unit | Title | Narration | Parts |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neighbours (Tim Winton) | 8.5 min | cold open (a shouting street mistaken for murder), 6 parts (Winton and 1980s multicultural Australia; the year season by season on a "closeness" curve; the plot as the things that cross the fence; the characters and why nobody has a name; four themes and the ironic last line; free indirect style, seasons as structure, devices), 2 quizzes, recap |
| 2 | A Respectable Woman (Kate Chopin) | 8.1 min | cold open ("I have overcome everything!" — two readings), 6 parts (Chopin and local colour; the story walked along its classical plot curve; the bench: Whitman, the voice, the impulse meter and the antithesis; Mrs. Baroda, Gouvernail, Gaston and the symbols; desire vs respectability and three more themes; three readings of the ending and the devices), 2 quizzes, recap |
| 3 | A Devoted Son (Anita Desai) | 7.8 min | cold open (feet touched in respect, then tucked away), 6 parts (Desai; the great ascent as a staircase, with the "USA" satire; the hinge, the crossed-off foods, the tonics, the last evening and the gate; Rakesh, Varma, Veena, Bhatia; duty vs will, care vs control and more; two halves and a narrator who changes sides, the three ironies, devices), 2 quizzes, recap |
| 4 | The Treasure in the Forest (H. G. Wells) | 6.6 min | cold open (Chang-hi's map and its little dashes), 6 parts (Wells; Evans's dream: galleon → Chang-hi → overheard → seized; glare to gloom, the corpse and the gold, three little pricks; the dashes turn into thorns, the falling petals; Evans, Hooker, Chang-hi and four themes; every clue planted early, devices), 2 quizzes, recap |

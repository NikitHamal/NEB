6.3 Young's double slit

Two coherent sources produce bright and dark fringes where the path difference is a whole or half number of wavelengths. The fringe width depends on the screen distance and the slit separation.

β = λD / d
Check yourself

If the slit separation is halved, the fringe width:

AnswerB. β is inversely proportional to d, so halving d doubles β.

Why labs failed, and the fix

Never a dead panelThe engine and scene are fetched with two quiet retries. After 8 seconds the wait is explained, and a failure shows Try again, not "refresh the page".
Fullscreen always opensThe stage detaches from any transformed parent, resizes the canvas twice (layout, then measure), hides the system bars and exits on Back, the arrow or the close button.
Honest about the deviceNo WebGL means a clear message and the lesson text, not a spinner. The renderer pauses with the screen and is freed when you leave.

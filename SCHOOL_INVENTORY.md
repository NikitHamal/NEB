## PDF pipeline (reused, not invented)

- Source: `notes/content/<slug>/unit-NN.md` + spine `notes/curriculum/<slug>.md` (spine is the authority; `title/hours/area` frontmatter must equal it).
- Build: `cd notes/pipeline && python3 build_pdf.py <slug>` → `notes/pdf/NEBians-<Subject>-Grade-<N>.pdf`. Validate a chapter: `python3 check.py ../content/<slug>/unit-NN.md`; all chapters: `python3 audit.py`.
- Requires **Python ≥ 3.12** (`md2html.py` uses PEP 701 f-strings; fails with `SyntaxError` on 3.11), WeasyPrint + Pango, matplotlib, pymupdf, and the five fonts from `notes/pipeline/fonts/install.sh` (not vendored; building without them silently falls back to wrong fonts).
- No `qa_pdf.py` exists in this repo; QA = `check.py`/`audit.py` + render-and-look (`pdftoppm`) + `pdftotext` diff against the previous PDF.

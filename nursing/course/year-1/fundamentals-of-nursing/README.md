# Fundamentals of Nursing (BSN 4 theory, BSN 5 practical)

Year 1, BSc Nursing (Tribhuvan University IOM and affiliated Nepal campuses). Theory 100 h, clinical 510 h. Marks: 100 (theory) + 100 (practical).

| Unit | Title | Content area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nursing as a Profession | Definitions, Nightingale, history of nursing in Nepal, roles of the nurse, NNC Act 2052, Code of Ethics |
| 2 | Nursing Process and Care Planning | Assess-Diagnose-Plan-Implement-Evaluate, NANDA-I, NOC, NIC, documentation, Braden scale and pressure injury |
| 3 | Vital Signs and Health Assessment | Temperature, pulse, respiration, blood pressure, SpO2, MAP, BMI, GCS |
| 4 | Infection Prevention and Control | Chain of infection, hand hygiene, PPE, standard precautions, chlorine solutions, sharps and waste |
| 5 | Medication Administration and Clinical Calculations | Rights, routes, injections, IV cannulation, dose and drip-rate sums |

## Verification

- Every number (workload, Braden score, MAP, BMI, temperature conversion, GCS, chlorine dilution, needle-stick risk, dosage, drip rate, BSA) is computed by `nursing/tools/check_fundamentals.py`; the Python blocks printed in Units 3 and 5 are executed by the same script and compared with the printed output.
- Photographs are real, open-licensed Wikimedia Commons files registered through `notes/pipeline/assets.py` (licence-gated; credits are generated in the Picture Credits appendix).

## Source and honesty

Course title, hours and marks follow the IOM/TU BSN curriculum table (BSN 4, BSN 5) as reproduced on importantedunotes.com and the NAMS BSN curriculum. **The unit headings are reconstructed from secondary sources and standard texts (Kozier and Erb, Potter and Perry, WHO guidelines, NNC documents); check them against your campus copy of the IOM curriculum.** Doses and protocols are teaching examples; always follow your hospital's protocol and the prescriber's order.

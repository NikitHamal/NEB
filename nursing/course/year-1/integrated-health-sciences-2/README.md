# Integrated Health Sciences II (BSN 2-3)

Year 1, BSc Nursing (Tribhuvan University IOM and affiliated Nepal campuses). Theory 150 h plus practical 120 h. Session 3 wrote five units on the remaining body systems.

| Unit | Title | Content area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Nervous System and Special Senses | Brain regions (real MRI), neuron and action potential, reflex arc, ICP, CPP, GCS, mannitol dose, eye, Snellen, refraction, ear, stroke education |
| 2 | The Endocrine System and Diabetes | Hormones and feedback, thyroid (real micrograph), iodine, HPA axis, pancreas (real micrograph), HbA1c to eAG, insulin dose, DKA |
| 3 | Renal System, Fluids, Electrolytes and Acid-Base | Nephron (real micrograph), GFR, Cockcroft-Gault, ADH, RAAS, fluid compartments, 4-2-1 rule, electrolytes, ABG, CKD with hyperkalaemia |
| 4 | Reproductive System, Menstrual Cycle and Early Pregnancy | Testis and ovary micrographs, cycle hormones, ovulation day, Naegele and 280-day EDD, gestational age, Pearl Index, family planning, anaemia in pregnancy |
| 5 | Musculoskeletal and Integumentary Systems | Bone micrograph, knee X-ray, skin micrograph, burn photo, fracture healing, rule of nines, Parkland, Curreri, T-score, crutch length, major burns |

## Verification

- Every number is computed by `nursing/tools/check_ihs2.py` (75 tokens, 5 executed Python blocks, extra asserts); run with `--verify`.
- Sixteen real/illustrative images, open-licensed (Wikimedia Commons, slug `nur2`, credits auto-generated); one (glucometer) is a labelled illustration, not a photograph.

## Source and honesty

Course title and hours follow the IOM/TU BSN table as reproduced on importantedunotes.com and the NAMS BSN curriculum. **Unit headings are reconstructed from secondary sources and standard texts (Guyton and Hall, Tortora, Ross and Wilson, Kozier and Erb, NANDA-I, NIC, NOC, WHO, MoHP, NDHS 2022); check them against your campus IOM curriculum.** Doses are teaching examples; follow the prescriber and hospital protocol.

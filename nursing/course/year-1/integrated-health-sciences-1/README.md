# Integrated Health Sciences I (BSN 1)

Year 1, BSc Nursing (Tribhuvan University IOM and affiliated Nepal campuses). Theory 150 h (anatomy, physiology, biochemistry and pathophysiology of the first body systems). Session 2 wrote five units on the systems the first-year student meets earliest; Integrated Health Sciences II (BSN 2-3) will carry the remaining systems.

| Unit | Title | Content area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cell, Tissues, Homeostasis and Body Chemistry | Cell, membrane transport, tonicity, Nernst, mitosis, four tissues (micrographs), pH and bicarbonate buffer, IV fluid care |
| 2 | Blood, Haemostasis and Immunity | Blood composition, haemopoiesis, red-cell indices, anaemia (Nepal programme), coagulation, ABO and Rh, transfusion nursing, innate and adaptive immunity |
| 3 | The Cardiovascular System and ECG | Heart structure, conduction, cardiac cycle, cardiac output, MAP, SVR, RAAS, ECG interval and rate arithmetic, auscultation, PEN hypertension education |
| 4 | The Respiratory System, Gas Transport and Acid-Base | Airway, mechanics, spirometry, ventilation arithmetic, oxyhaemoglobin curve, altitude, ABG interpretation, oxygen therapy |
| 5 | Digestion, Metabolism and Energy | Gut wall, stomach, villus, liver, enzymes, BMR and TEE, glucose units, ORS physiology, NG-tube care, peptic ulcer |

## Source and honesty

Course title, hours and marks follow the IOM/TU BSN curriculum table (BSN 1) as reproduced on importantedunotes.com and the NAMS BSN curriculum. **The unit headings are reconstructed from secondary sources and standard texts (Guyton and Hall, Tortora, Ross and Wilson, Marieb, Harper's Biochemistry, WHO, MoHP/NDHS 2022); check them against your campus copy of the IOM curriculum.** Doses and protocols are teaching examples; always follow your hospital's protocol and the prescriber's order.

# Nursing Concepts (BSN 15)

Year 2, BSc Nursing (Tribhuvan University IOM and affiliated Nepal campuses). Theory 60 h, 50 marks. Session 10 wrote six units (PDF built with `nebpdf.py`, 60 pages, `qa_pdf.py` fails=0 warns=0).

| Unit | Title | Syllabus units covered |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Health, Illness, Stress and Crisis | 1 Health and illness (6 h) and 2 Stress and crisis (8 h): WHO and Dunn, continuum, prevention levels, Suchman, convalescence tasks, GAS and LAS, Lazarus, Holmes-Rahe, crisis types and phases, Kubler-Ross, mourning in Nepal; case: young widow in crisis |
| 2 | Pain | 3 Pain (6 h): pathway, gate control, factors, PQRST, NRS, FACES, FLACC, WHO ladder, opioid maths (1/6 rule, oral:SC 2:1, fentanyl, PCA), palliative care in Nepal; case: cancer bone pain |
| 3 | Communication and Physical Examination | 4 Communication (6 h) plus the course objective on physical examination: loop, SOLER, barriers, Peplau, SBAR, four techniques, MAP, BMI, WHR; case: Maithili-speaking cardiac patient |
| 4 | Nursing Theories and Models | 5 Theories (8 h): Nightingale (Scutari 42.7% to 2.2%), Henderson 14, Abdellah 21, Orem, Neuman; case: Orem and diabetic foot with insulin maths |
| 5 | Holistic Nursing, Complementary Therapies, Ayurveda and Creativity | 6 Holistic (8 h) and 8 Creativity (8 h): TT, Reiki, acupuncture, biofeedback, relaxation, yoga, Ayurveda and DoAAM, Wallas, WHO hand-rub formula; case: stress and tension headache |
| 6 | Rehabilitation, Ethics and Law | 7 Rehabilitation (6 h) and 9 Ethical and legal aspects (4 h): rehab cycle, aids, Barthel, Act 2074, consent, negligence, NNC Act 2052, Criminal Code ss. 230-232; case: post-stroke rehabilitation |

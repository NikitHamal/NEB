# Psychiatric Nursing (BSN 13-14)

Year 2, BSc Nursing (Tribhuvan University IOM and affiliated Nepal campuses). Theory 70 h plus practical 90 h. Session 9 wrote five units (PDF built with `nebpdf.py`, `qa_pdf.py` fails=0).

| Unit | Title | Content area |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Foundations of Psychiatric Nursing | WHO mental health definition, biopsychosocial model, NMHS 2020 burden and treatment gap, history (Pinel, Bedlam, Kirkbride, Peplau), ICD-11 and DSM-5-TR, Nepal service pyramid and mhGAP, Peplau phases, therapeutic communication, Mental Status Examination, defence mechanisms, ethics and law (no standalone Mental Health Act in Nepal); case: withdrawn, mistrustful young man |
| 2 | Schizophrenia and Antipsychotic Drugs | Positive, negative, cognitive symptoms, Schneider and Bleuler, DSM-5-TR criteria, dopamine pathways, antipsychotic classes and doses, chlorpromazine equivalents, depot volumes, EPS timeline, NMS versus serotonin syndrome, metabolic monitoring, AIMS; case: first-episode psychosis with acute dystonia |
| 3 | Mood Disorders and Suicide | Depression, PHQ-9, monoamine synapse, mania and bipolar, fluoxetine, amitriptyline overdose dose, lithium levels, ECT nursing, Nepal Police suicide data FY 2080/81, crude rate, Section 185, mhGAP risk pathway; case: moderate-to-high suicide risk with pesticide at home |
| 4 | Anxiety, OCD, Trauma and Dissociative Disorders | Peplau anxiety levels, GAD-7, panic, phobias, amygdala, benzodiazepine equivalents, Y-BOCS and ritual time, PCL-5, psychological first aid, conversion disorder and non-epileptic seizures, personality and childhood disorders; case: panic disorder with avoidance |
| 5 | Substance Use, Psychiatric Emergencies and Community Psychiatry | Dependence criteria, stages of change, standard drinks and AUDIT, CIWA-Ar, diazepam regimen, thiamine, naloxone, COWS, methadone volume, agitation and rapid tranquillisation, restraint rules, mhGAP community care and rehabilitation; case: delirium tremens |

## Source and honesty

**Unit headings inside each course are reconstructed from secondary sources (importantedunotes.com, NAMS BSN curriculum PDF) and standard texts; verify against your campus copy of the IOM curriculum.** Facts used (searched in session 9): National Mental Health Survey Nepal 2020 (NHRC; 9,200 adults and 5,888 adolescents; lifetime 10.0%, current 4.3%, adolescents 5.2%, current suicidal thoughts 6.5%, lifetime attempt 1.1%, 23% sought treatment, neurotic and stress-related 3% adults and 2.8% adolescents); about 1.5 psychiatric beds per 100,000; National Mental Health Strategy and Action Plan 2020 (2021-2025) and Community Mental Health Care Package 2017 (mhGAP-aligned); **Nepal has no standalone Mental Health Act** (Disability Rights Act 2074 sections 35-36 give some provisions); Muluki Criminal Code 2074 section 185 punishes abetting suicide; Nepal Police FY 2080/81: 7,221 suicidal deaths, about 85% hanging, 56% men, 33% women, 8% girls, 4% boys (rounded); national suicide helpline 1166 (confirm current numbers); WHO schizophrenia about 1 in 300; mhGAP-IG 2.0 priority conditions. Scales (PHQ-9, GAD-7, Y-BOCS, PCL-5, AUDIT, CIWA-Ar, COWS, AIMS) follow their published cut-offs; chlorpromazine and benzodiazepine equivalents are approximate (Gardner 2010, BNF). Follow your hospital protocol and the current MoHP standard treatment guideline for doses.

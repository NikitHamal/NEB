Search and Filter concept

What changed. The old sheet put eleven subjects in one wall of grey pills under a tagline. Now: sort is one tap on a single row, each group collapses, subjects can be searched, and the main button counts the results live. Search keeps recents and popular words, and adds scopes (Resources, Posts, People, Schools) as a tab bar with counts that never truncate. Applied filters sit under the tabs as removable chips.

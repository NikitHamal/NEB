# AREA: Physics (27)
## 1. Mechanics [8]
- Physical quantities, vectors and kinematics: dimensions, resolution and polygon law, vector algebra, equations of motion, projectile motion, relative motion
- Newton's laws of motion and friction: conservation of linear momentum, equilibrium and non-equilibrium applications, laws of solid friction
- Work, energy and power: work-energy theorem, kinetic and potential energy, conservation of energy, conservative and non-conservative forces, elastic and inelastic collisions
- Circular motion, gravitation and SHM: centripetal force, conical pendulum, banking, gravitational potential, variation of g, satellites, rocket launch, energy in SHM, spring-mass system, simple pendulum, damped and forced oscillation, resonance
- Rotational dynamics: moment of inertia, radius of gyration, rotational KE, centre of gravity and mass, torque, conservation of angular momentum
- Elasticity: Hooke's law, Young modulus, bulk modulus, modulus of rigidity, Poisson's ratio, elastic energy
- Fluid mechanics: buoyancy, flotation, Archimedes' principle, surface tension, capillarity, viscosity, Newton, Stokes and Poiseuille formulae, Reynolds number, continuity, Bernoulli
## 2. Heat and Thermodynamics [4]
- Temperature and quantity of heat: thermal equilibrium, specific heat, latent heat, method of mixture, Newton's law of cooling, triple point
- Thermal expansion of solids and liquids
- Transfer of heat: conduction, convection, radiation, thermal conductivity, black body, Stefan-Boltzmann law
- Thermal properties of matter: kinetic theory of gases, heat capacities of gases and solids
- Laws of thermodynamics: first law, processes, second law, heat engine, Carnot, Otto and Diesel cycles, refrigerator, entropy
## 3. Geometric and Physical Optics [4]
- Reflection: plane and curved mirrors, mirror formula
- Refraction: plane surface, critical angle, total internal reflection, lateral shift, prism, minimum deviation, lenses, lens maker's formula, combination of lenses, optical fibre
- Dispersion: spectrum, dispersive power, chromatic and spherical aberration, achromatism, scattering of light
- Nature and propagation of light: Huygens' principle, velocity of light
- Interference: coherent sources, Young's double slit experiment
- Diffraction: Fraunhofer diffraction, grating, resolving power
- Polarization: Brewster's law, transverse nature, Polaroid
## 4. Waves and Sound [2]
- Wave motion: travelling and stationary waves
- Mechanical waves: velocity of sound in solid, liquid and gas; effect of temperature, pressure, humidity
- Waves in pipes and strings: closed and open pipes, resonance tube, laws of vibration of a string
- Acoustic phenomena: pressure amplitude, intensity level, quality and pitch, ultrasonic and infrasonic, Doppler effect
## 5. Electricity and Magnetism [7]
- Electrostatics: Coulomb's law, field, Gauss's law, potential, capacitors and their combinations, dielectrics, energy stored
- DC circuits: Ohm's law, resistivity, work and power, galvanometer and ohmmeter, internal resistance, Joule's law, Kirchhoff's laws
- Thermoelectric effect: Seebeck, thermocouples, Peltier, thermopile, Thomson
- Magnetic effect: force on a conductor and a charge, torque, Hall effect, Biot-Savart law, Ampere's law, force between parallel conductors
- Magnetic properties of matter: earth's magnetism, magnetic materials, permeability, susceptibility, hysteresis
- Electromagnetic induction: Faraday's law, induced emf, AC generator, self and mutual induction, energy in an inductor, transformer
- Alternating currents: rms value, phasors of R, L, C, quality factor, power factor
## 6. Modern Physics [2]
- Electrons: Millikan's experiment, cathode rays, specific charge
- Photons and quantization of energy: photoelectric effect, Planck's constant, Bohr theory, spectral series, de Broglie, uncertainty principle, X-rays and Bragg's law, laser
- Solids and semiconductor devices: intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors, p-n junction, rectification, Zener diode, transistor, logic gates
- Radioactivity and nuclear reactions: atomic mass, isotopes, nuclear density, mass-energy relation, mass defect, fission and fusion, radioactive decay law, carbon dating, health hazard
- Recent trends: particle physics, the universe, seismology, telecommunication, environment, new technology and materials

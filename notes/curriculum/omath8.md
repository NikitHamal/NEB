# SUBJECT: Optional Mathematics | GRADE: 8 | CODE: Optional (Basic Level)
# EXAM: School annual examination, 2 hr, FM 50 written | Group A objective 10x1=10 | Group B short 10x2=20 | Group C long 5x4=20
# SOURCE: CDC Optional Mathematics, Grade 8 — scope and sequence of subject matter (Algebra, Limit, Matrix, Coordinate Geometry, Trigonometry, Vector, Transformation, Statistics). Units follow that sequence; the three Algebra strands (relation and function, polynomials, sequence and series) are printed as three units because each is taught and examined on its own.
# NOTE: optional mathematics at the basic level is examined by the school, not by a national board, so there is no national specification grid; the practice sets are written to the 50-mark school paper above, the same shape as the grade 8 compulsory mathematics book, so a student meets one pattern across both papers.
## 1. Relation and Function
- Ordered pairs and equal ordered pairs
- Cartesian product of two sets
- Relation: meaning, representation (ordered pairs, table, arrow diagram, graph), domain and range, inverse relation
- Function: meaning and notation, domain, co-domain and range, image and pre-image
- Representation of a function and the vertical line test
## 2. Polynomials
- Polynomial expressions and polynomial functions; classification by degree and by number of terms
- Degree of a polynomial, standard form and equal polynomials
- Addition and subtraction of polynomials
- Multiplication of polynomials
## 3. Sequence and Series
- Sequence: meaning, terms and the general term
- Finding terms and the rule of a sequence
- Series and the sigma (Σ) notation
## 4. Limit
- Concept of limit from sequences of numbers
- Concept of limit from sequences of diagrams
- Value of a function and its limiting value
- Notation of limit
## 5. Matrix
- Matrix: elements, rows, columns and order
- Types of matrices: row, column, zero, rectangular, square, diagonal, scalar, unit, equal, symmetric and triangular
- Addition and subtraction of matrices and the properties of matrix addition
- Multiplication of a matrix by a scalar
## 6. Coordinate Geometry
- Distance formula and its applications
- Section formula: dividing a line segment in a given ratio; mid-point
- Slope of a line; lines parallel to the axes; slope-intercept form y = mx + c
- Area of a triangle and a quadrilateral from the coordinates of the vertices
## 7. Trigonometry
- Measurement of angles: sexagesimal, centesimal and circular (radian) systems
- Trigonometric ratios of an acute angle
- Trigonometric identities and their conversions
- Values of trigonometric ratios of 0°, 30°, 45°, 60° and 90°
- Trigonometric ratios of (90° ± θ), (180° ± θ) and (−θ)
- Height and distance with one angle of elevation or depression
## 8. Vector
- Vector and scalar quantities; magnitude and direction of a vector
- Types of vectors: row, column, position, unit, null, equal and negative vectors; like and unlike vectors
- Multiplication of a vector by a scalar; condition for parallel vectors
- Addition and subtraction of vectors
## 9. Transformation
- Transformation: meaning; isometric and non-isometric transformations
- Reflection in the x-axis and the y-axis
- Rotation about the origin through 90°, 180°, 270° and 360°
- Translation by a vector
## 10. Statistics
- Arithmetic mean of ungrouped and grouped data
- Median and quartiles of ungrouped data
- Mode of ungrouped and grouped data
- Range and coefficient of range

# PART: Botany
## 1. Plant Anatomy [8]
- Concept of tissues: plant tissue systems (epidermal, ground and vascular tissue systems)
- Meristems and permanent tissues: apical, intercalary and lateral meristems; parenchyma, collenchyma, sclerenchyma, xylem, phloem and secretory tissues
- Anatomy of dicot and monocot root: internal structure (T.S.) and comparison of dicot and monocot roots
- Anatomy of dicot and monocot stem: internal structure (T.S.) of dicot (*Helianthus*) and monocot (*Zea mays*) stems and types of vascular bundles
- Anatomy of dicot and monocot leaf: dorsiventral (dicot) and isobilateral (monocot) leaf anatomy and Kranz anatomy
- Secondary growth of dicot stem: vascular cambium ring formation, secondary xylem and phloem, annual rings, heartwood and sapwood, cork cambium (phellogen) and periderm
## 2. Plant Physiology [20]
- Water relation: diffusion, imbibition, osmosis, water potential ($\Psi_w = \Psi_s + \Psi_p$), turgor, plasmolysis, water absorption, root pressure, ascent of sap, transpiration, stomatal mechanism and guttation
- Photosynthesis: chloroplast ultrastructure, photosynthetic pigments, cyclic and non-cyclic photophosphorylation, Calvin ($C_3$) cycle, photorespiration, Hatch–Slack ($C_4$) pathway, CAM plants and Blackman's law of limiting factors
- Respiration: glycolysis, oxidative decarboxylation (link reaction), Krebs (TCA) cycle, electron transport chain, oxidative phosphorylation, ATP balance sheet, anaerobic fermentation and respiratory quotient (RQ)
- Plant hormones: physiological roles and agricultural applications of auxins, gibberellins, cytokinins, abscisic acid (ABA) and ethylene
- Plant growth and movement: phases and sigmoid curve of growth, seed dormancy, vernalization, photoperiodism, and tropic, nastic and tactic plant movements
## 3. Genetics [21]
- Genetic Materials: Griffith, Avery and Hershey–Chase experiments, DNA and RNA chemical structure, Watson–Crick double helix model, semi-conservative DNA replication, central dogma (transcription, genetic code, translation) and concept of gene
- Mendelian genetics: Mendel's monohybrid and dihybrid crosses, laws of dominance, segregation and independent assortment, test and back cross, incomplete dominance, codominance, multiple alleles, pleiotropy, polygenic inheritance, sex determination, sex-linked inheritance and pedigree analysis
- Linkage and crossing over: complete and incomplete linkage (Morgan's *Drosophila* experiments), mechanism and significance of crossing over, recombination frequency and chromosome mapping
- Mutation and polyploidy: gene (point/frameshift) and chromosomal mutations, mutagens, euploidy (autopolyploidy and allopolyploidy) and aneuploidy (trisomy and monosomy)
## 4. Embryology [8]
- Asexual and sexual reproduction in angiosperms: vegetative propagation methods (cutting, layering, grafting) and sexual flower structure
- Pollination: self-pollination (autogamy, geitonogamy) and cross-pollination (xenogamy), contrivances for cross-pollination and pollinating agents (anemophily, hydrophily, entomophily, ornithophily)
- Fertilization: pollen–pistil interaction, pollen tube growth (porogamy, chalazogamy, mesogamy), syngamy, triple fusion and double fertilization
- Development of male gametophyte: anther wall layers, tapetum, microsporogenesis and development of 2-celled/3-celled male gametophyte
- Development of female gametophyte: ovule structure and types, megasporogenesis and monosporic 7-celled 8-nucleate (*Polygonum* type) embryo sac development
- Dicot and monocot embryo: embryogeny in dicot (*Capsella bursa-pastoris*) and monocot (*Sagittaria*/grass) embryos
- Endosperm: nuclear, cellular and helobial endosperm types, ruminant endosperm and fate of floral parts after fertilization
## 5. Biotechnology [7]
- Tissue culture: totipotency, nutrient media (MS medium), sterilization, callus, suspension, meristem, embryo, anther/haploid culture, somatic hybridization and micropropagation
- Plant breeding: plant introduction, acclimatization, selection (mass, pure-line, clonal), hybridization steps, heterosis (hybrid vigour) and mutation breeding
- Disease-resistant plants: breeding and biotechnological approaches for pest and pathogen resistance (including *Bt* crops)
- Green manure, bio-fertilizer, bio-pesticide: leguminous green manuring, *Rhizobium*, *Azotobacter*, *Azospirillum*, *Azolla–Anabaena*, mycorrhiza, *Bacillus thuringiensis* and neem-based biopesticides
- Genetic engineering and GMOs: restriction endonucleases, ligases, plasmid and bacteriophage vectors, recombinant DNA steps, PCR, gel electrophoresis, transgenic plants/animals and biosafety
- Bio-engineering: vegetative barriers, check dams, fascines, brush layering and slope stabilization using deep-rooted plants in Nepal
- Food safety and food security: pillars of food security, food adulteration, mycotoxins, pesticide residues, food preservation and quality standards
# PART: Zoology
## 6. Animal Tissues [8]
- Epithelial tissue: simple (squamous, cuboidal, columnar, ciliated, pseudostratified), compound (stratified and transitional) and exocrine/endocrine glandular epithelia
- Connective tissue: loose (areolar, adipose), dense (tendon, ligament), skeletal (hyaline, elastic and fibrous cartilage; compact and spongy bone; Haversian system) and fluid (blood and lymph) connective tissues
- Muscular tissue: striated (skeletal sarcomere ultrastructure), non-striated (smooth) and cardiac muscle structure and properties
- Nervous tissue: multipolar, bipolar and unipolar neurons, myelinated and non-myelinated nerve fibres, synapses and neuroglia
## 7. Developmental Biology [6]
- Gametogenesis: spermatogenesis (spermatocytogenesis and spermiogenesis), oogenesis, structure of human sperm and ovum, and types of eggs based on yolk amount and distribution
- Development of frog: fertilization, cleavage, morula, blastula and blastulation, gastrulation (epiboly and emboly), neurulation, fate of three germ layers, organogenesis and metamorphosis
## 8. Human Biology [28]
- Digestive system: alimentary canal histology, dental formula, salivary glands, liver, pancreas, gastrointestinal hormones, enzymatic digestion and villus absorption
- Respiratory System: respiratory tract, lungs, mechanism of inspiration and expiration, lung volumes and capacities, oxygen dissociation curve, Bohr and Haldane effects, and chloride shift
- Circulatory System: blood composition, ABO and Rh blood groups, heart wall and valves, SA and AV nodes, conducting system, cardiac cycle, heart sounds, ECG, blood pressure and double circulation
- Excretory System: kidney gross and nephron structure, counter-current multiplier, ultrafiltration, selective reabsorption, tubular secretion, osmoregulation by ADH and RAAS
- Nervous system: nerve impulse resting and action potentials, saltatory conduction, cholinergic synaptic transmission, brain divisions (cerebrum, diencephalon, midbrain, pons, cerebellum, medulla), spinal cord, cranial/spinal nerves and reflex arc
- Sense organs: structure and working of human eye (retina rods and cones, accommodation, visual defects) and human ear (cochlea organ of Corti for hearing, vestibular apparatus for equilibrium)
- Endocrinology: hypothalamus, pituitary (adenohypophysis and neurohypophysis), thyroid, parathyroid, adrenal cortex and medulla, pancreatic islets of Langerhans, gonads, pineal and thymus hormones, mechanisms and hypo-/hyper-secretion disorders
- Reproductive System: male and female reproductive organs, histology of testis and ovary, menstrual cycle phases and hormonal control, fertilization, implantation, placenta and parturition
## 9. Human Population and Health Disorders [6]
- Human Population: natality, mortality, J-shaped and S-shaped growth curves, age–sex pyramids, demographic transition, population trends in Nepal and family planning/contraceptive methods
- Health disorders: communicable diseases (typhoid, tuberculosis, ascariasis, giardiasis, malaria, HIV/AIDS), innate and adaptive immunity, vaccines, non-communicable cardiovascular/cancer disorders, and drug, alcohol and smoking abuse
## 10. Applied Biology [16]
- Application of Zoology: apiculture, sericulture, pisciculture, poultry farming, dairy/livestock management, vermicomposting, and human organ/corneal transplantation, amniocentesis and IVF
- Microbial diseases and application of microbiology: bacterial, viral, protozoan and fungal human diseases, modes of transmission, diagnostic tests (ELISA, Widal, RT-PCR), antibiotics, vaccines and industrial/medical microbiology

# AREA: Mathematics (35)
## 1. Set, Logic and Functions [3]
- Set, real number system, intervals, absolute value, logic, connectives, laws of logic
- Function, types of functions: injective, surjective, bijective, algebraic, trigonometric, exponential and logarithmic; inverse of a function, composite functions
## 2. Algebra [8]
- Matrices and determinants, types and properties, inverse of a matrix
- Complex numbers and polynomial equations
- Sequence and series, permutation and combination
- Binomial theorem, exponential and logarithmic series
## 3. Trigonometry [4]
- Trigonometric equations and general values
- Inverse trigonometric functions, principal value
- Properties of triangles, in-centre, ortho-centre and circum-centre, solution of triangles
## 4. Coordinate Geometry [6]
- Straight lines, pair of lines
- Circles, equations of circle in different forms, tangent and normal
- Conic sections: parabola, ellipse and hyperbola, standard equations and simple properties
- Coordinates in space, plane and its equation
## 5. Calculus [8]
- Limit and continuity of functions, indeterminate forms, L'Hospital's rule
- Derivatives, rules of derivatives, geometrical and physical meanings, higher order derivatives, applications of derivative: tangent and normal, rate of change, maxima and minima
- Integration, linear properties, rules of integration, standard integrals, definite integral, applications of definite integral: area under a curve and area between two curves
- Differential equations: definition, order and degree, first order and first degree: variable separable, homogeneous, linear and exact equations, integrating factor
## 6. Vectors and their Products [3]
- Vectors in plane and space, algebra of vectors, linear combination of vectors, linearly dependent and independent sets of vectors
- Product of two vectors, scalar and vector product of two vectors, scalar triple product
## 7. Statistics and Probability [3]
- Measures of location and measures of dispersion
- Correlation and regression
- Basic terms of probability, conditional and compound probability, additive and multiplicative rules, Bayes' theorem, binomial distribution

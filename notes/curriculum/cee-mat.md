# AREA: Mental Agility Test (20)
## 1. Verbal Reasoning [5]
- Direction sense and distance
- Blood relations
- Coding and decoding
- Analogy and classification (odd one out)
- Ranking, ordering and seating arrangement
- Syllogisms and statement–conclusion reasoning
## 2. Numerical Reasoning [5]
- Percentages, ratio and proportion, averages
- Profit and loss, simple and compound interest
- Time and work, speed, distance and time
- Ages, mixtures and number properties
- Fast calculation and estimation
## 3. Logical Sequencing [5]
- Number series
- Letter and alphanumeric series
- Patterns in grids and missing-number figures
- Calendar and clock reasoning
## 4. Spatial Relation and Abstract Reasoning [5]
- Nets, cubes and dice
- Mirror and water images
- Paper folding and cutting
- Embedded figures and counting figures
- Figure series, analogies and matrices

# SUBJECT: English | TRACK: IOE (BE/BArch) | QUESTIONS: 16 | SYLLABUS: IOE Detailed Syllabus of B.E./B.Arch. Entrance Examination-2083 (English, full marks 20)
# WEIGHTAGE: Subject total (20 marks) official; the [n] split is the NEBians estimate (12 one-mark + 4 two-mark = 16 questions = 20 marks).
# AREA: English (16)
## 1. Grammar I [4]
- Sequence of tenses, concord (agreement)
- Direct and indirect speech
- Kinds of sentences and transformation of sentences
## 2. Grammar II [6]
- Basic grammatical patterns and structures, conditional sentences
- Parts of speech, active and passive voice
- Verbals: infinitives, gerunds and participles
- Punctuation and use of prepositions
- Vocabulary
- Idiomatic expressions
## 3. Phonetics [2]
- Phonemes (vowels and consonants)
- Syllables and stress (word and sentence)
## 4. Comprehension [4]
- Comprehension of reading passages on a variety of topics and styles, with special reference to General English and Technical English

# AREA: Physical Chemistry (17)
## 1. Basic Concepts and Stoichiometry [2]
- Atoms, molecules, valency, radicals; relative atomic and molecular mass; the atomic mass unit
- Chemical equations, empirical and molecular formulae, percentage composition
- Dalton's atomic theory, the laws of stoichiometry and Avogadro's law
- The mole concept, limiting reactant and percentage yield
## 2. Atomic Structure [2]
- Rutherford's and Bohr's atomic models; the spectrum of hydrogen
- de Broglie's equation and Heisenberg's uncertainty principle
- Orbitals and quantum numbers; the Aufbau principle, Pauli's exclusion principle and Hund's rule; electronic configuration
## 3. Classification of Elements and Periodicity [1]
- Modern periodic law and table; s, p, d and f blocks; isoelectronic species
- Periodic trends: atomic and ionic size, ionization energy, electronegativity, electron affinity and metallic character
## 4. Chemical Bonding and Shapes of Molecules [2]
- Electronic theory of valency; ionic, covalent and coordinate bonds; Lewis structures of s- and p-block compounds
- VSEPR theory and the shapes of simple molecules; valence bond theory, sigma and pi bonds, hybridization
- Dipole moment, ionic character of covalent bonds and bond length
- Hydrogen bonding, metallic bonding and van der Waals forces
## 5. Redox Reactions [1]
- Classical and electronic concepts of oxidation and reduction; oxidation number
- Balancing redox equations by the oxidation-number and ion-electron methods; applications
## 6. States of Matter [2]
- Gases: kinetic theory, the gas laws, the ideal and combined gas equations, deviation of real gases
- Liquids: vapour pressure, boiling point, surface tension, viscosity, liquid crystals, solubility and solubility curves
- Solids: crystalline and amorphous solids; efflorescent, deliquescent and hygroscopic substances; water of crystallization
- The unit cell, the seven crystal systems and the fourteen Bravais lattices; types of crystalline solid by bonding
## 7. Chemical and Ionic Equilibrium [2]
- Physical and chemical equilibrium; the law of mass action; Kc, Kp, the reaction quotient and the Kp–Kc relation
- Le Chatelier's principle and its applications
- Arrhenius, Brønsted–Lowry and Lewis acids and bases; Ostwald's dilution law
- Ionic product of water, pH, pOH, pKa and pKb; common-ion effect; solubility product
- Acidic and basic buffers; types of salts and their hydrolysis
## 8. Volumetric Analysis [1]
- Equivalent weight; concentration as percentage, g/L, normality, molarity, molality, formality, ppm, ppb and mole fraction
- Primary and secondary standards; the law of equivalence and the normality equation
## 9. Chemical Kinetics [1]
- Rate of reaction, rate expressions, the rate constant and its units; order and molecularity
- Integrated rate laws and half-lives of zero- and first-order reactions
- Collision theory, activation and threshold energy, the activated complex; factors affecting rate; homogeneous, heterogeneous and enzyme catalysis
## 10. Electrochemistry [1]
- Electrolytic cells and the qualitative and quantitative laws of electrolysis
- Standard electrode potential, the standard hydrogen and calomel electrodes, the electrochemical series and its uses
- Galvanic cells and their standard emf; primary, secondary and hydrogen–oxygen fuel cells
## 11. Chemical Thermodynamics [1]
- Systems and surroundings; state functions; internal energy; intensive and extensive properties; the first law and enthalpy
- Enthalpies of reaction, formation, combustion, neutralization, solution, fusion and vaporization; Laplace's and Hess's laws
- Spontaneity, entropy and the second law; Gibbs free energy and its relation to the equilibrium constant
## 12. Nuclear Chemistry [1]
- Radioactivity and types of nuclear reaction
- Radioisotopes and radiocarbon dating
# AREA: Inorganic Chemistry (10)
## 13. Chemistry of Non-metals [4]
- Atomic, molecular and nascent hydrogen; isotopes of hydrogen; heavy water; types of oxides
- Ozone: preparation, structure and tests; depletion of the ozone layer
- Ammonia, phosphine and nitric acid
- Chlorine, bromine and iodine; HCl, HBr and HI
- Allotropes of carbon and carbon monoxide; hydrogen sulphide, sulphur dioxide and sulphuric acid
## 14. Metallurgy and the Chemistry of Metals [4]
- Metallurgical principles: ores, gangue, flux, slag, concentration, calcination, roasting, smelting, bessemerization, aluminothermy, electrolytic reduction, poling, electro-refining and zone refining
- Alkali and alkaline-earth metals; sodium, sodium hydroxide and sodium carbonate
- 3d transition metals; shapes of complex ions; crystal field theory of octahedral complexes
- Copper, zinc, mercury, silver and iron: occurrence, extraction and properties; blue vitriol, white vitriol, calomel and corrosive sublimate
- Steel by the basic oxygen and open-hearth processes; corrosion of iron
## 15. Bio-inorganic Chemistry [2]
- Micro- and macronutrients; biological roles of sodium, potassium, magnesium, zinc, copper, cobalt, nickel, iron, chromium and calcium
- The sodium–potassium pump and the sodium–glucose pump
- Toxicity of iron, arsenic, mercury, lead and cadmium
# AREA: Organic Chemistry (17)
## 16. General Organic Chemistry [3]
- Tetravalency and catenation of carbon; classification of organic compounds; alkyl and aryl groups; functional groups and homologous series
- IUPAC nomenclature of aliphatic compounds; isomerism
- Homolytic and heterolytic fission; electrophiles, nucleophiles, carbocations, carbanions and free radicals; inductive and resonance effects
## 17. Hydrocarbons [3]
- Cracking, pyrolysis and reforming; gasoline quality, octane and cetane numbers, gasoline additives
- Alkanes, alkenes and alkynes: isomerism, nomenclature and preparation; properties of ethane; addition reactions of alkenes and alkynes; acidity of alkynes
- Aromaticity and resonance; preparation and properties of benzene
## 18. Haloalkanes, Haloarenes, Alcohols, Phenols and Ethers [4]
- Monohaloalkanes: nomenclature, isomerism, preparation and properties; SN1 and SN2 mechanisms
- Chloroform and chlorobenzene
- Monohydric alcohols: preparation and properties; oxo process, hydroboration–oxidation and fermentation; types of ethanol
- Phenol: preparation and properties
- Ethers: Williamson's synthesis and the properties of diethyl ether
## 19. Aldehydes, Ketones, Carboxylic Acids and their Derivatives [4]
- Aliphatic aldehydes and ketones: nomenclature, preparation and reactions; benzaldehyde
- Monocarboxylic acids: preparation and properties
- Acid halides, anhydrides, amides and esters: preparation, properties and relative reactivity
## 20. Nitro Compounds, Amines and Organometallics [3]
- Nitroalkanes and nitrobenzene
- Amines: classification, preparation of primary amines, basicity, Hofmann's separation; aniline
- Organolithium, organocopper and organocadmium compounds; the Grignard reagent
# AREA: Applied Chemistry (3)
## 21. Applied Chemistry and Chemistry in Service to Mankind [3]
- The chemical industry: stages of production, economics and plant management, batch and continuous processing, environmental effects and their control
- Cement, paper and pulp; nitric acid (Ostwald), ammonia (Haber), sulphuric acid (contact), caustic soda (diaphragm cell), sodium carbonate (Solvay) and urea
- Uses of the common non-metals, metals, inorganic compounds and organic compounds
- Polymers, dyes, drugs, pesticides and fertilizers; colloids, osmotic pressure and buffers in daily life; medical and industrial uses of radioisotopes
# AREA: Analytical Chemistry (3)
## 22. Analytical Chemistry [3]
- Tests for acid and basic radicals; tests for unsaturation and functional groups; distinction tests; Lassaigne's test; tests for fats, proteins and carbohydrates
- Separation techniques: filtration, sublimation, evaporation, precipitation, crystallization, distillation, paper chromatography and atmolysis
- Acid–base, redox (permanganometric, iodometric and iodimetric) and complexometric titrations; choice of indicator

# AREA: Mechanics (10)
## 1. Physical Quantities, Vectors and Kinematics [2]
- Precision, accuracy, significant figures and dimensional analysis
- Scalars and vectors: laws of addition, resolution, scalar and vector products
- Linear motion: equations of motion and their graphical treatment
- Projectile motion, with and without a resistive force
## 2. Dynamics: Force, Momentum, Work, Energy and Power [2]
- Newton's laws of motion and the conditions of equilibrium
- Impulse, linear momentum and its conservation
- Work, energy and power; the work–energy theorem; conservation of energy
- Elastic and inelastic collisions
- Solid friction: laws, coefficient, angle of friction and angle of repose
## 3. Rotational Dynamics [1]
- Moment of inertia of a uniform rod and radius of gyration
- Torque, rotational work, kinetic energy and power
- Angular momentum and its conservation
## 4. Fluid Statics and Dynamics [1]
- Pressure in a fluid; surface tension, surface energy and capillary action
- Viscosity: Newton's law of viscous flow, Stokes' law and terminal velocity, Poiseuille's formula
- Equation of continuity and Bernoulli's principle with applications
## 5. Circular Motion and Simple Harmonic Motion [2]
- Angular quantities; centripetal acceleration and force
- Motion in a horizontal and in a vertical circle
- Simple harmonic motion: displacement, velocity, acceleration, restoring force, energy, period and frequency
- Simple pendulum and spring–mass system; forced oscillation and resonance
## 6. Gravitation [1]
- Newton's law of gravitation; acceleration due to gravity and its variation
- Gravitational field strength, potential and potential energy
- Satellites: orbital velocity, period, energy, escape velocity, geostationary orbit
## 7. Elasticity [1]
- Stress, strain and Hooke's law
- Young's modulus, bulk modulus and modulus of rigidity; Poisson's ratio
- Elastic potential energy and energy density
# AREA: Heat and Thermodynamics (7)
## 8. Heat, Temperature and Heat Transfer [2]
- Thermal energy, heat and temperature; thermal equilibrium and the zeroth law
- Thermometers: liquid-in-glass, resistance and radiation thermometers — principle, advantages and limitations
- Conduction, convection and radiation; black-body radiation and the Stefan–Boltzmann law
## 9. Thermal Expansion and Quantity of Heat [2]
- Linear, superficial and cubical expansion and their relation
- Real and apparent expansion of liquids
- Heat capacity, specific heat capacity and the method of mixtures
- Latent heat of fusion and vaporization; the triple point
## 10. Ideal Gas and Thermodynamics [3]
- Ideal-gas equation; kinetic theory: pressure, rms speed and energy of molecules
- First law of thermodynamics: isothermal, adiabatic, isochoric and isobaric processes; molar heat capacities
- Second law of thermodynamics: internal-combustion engines, refrigerators and entropy
# AREA: Waves and Optics (8)
## 11. Waves and Sound [3]
- Progressive waves and the wave equation
- Speed of sound in solids, liquids and gases, and the factors that affect it
- Stationary waves: harmonics and overtones in strings and pipes
- Pressure amplitude, intensity and loudness, pitch and quality
- Doppler effect
## 12. Ray Optics: Reflection, Refraction and Dispersion [3]
- Reflection at curved mirrors: mirror formula and magnification
- Refraction at plane surfaces, critical angle and total internal reflection; refraction through a prism
- Lenses: lens formula, lens maker's formula, power and combinations
- Dispersion, chromatic aberration and achromatism
## 13. Wave Optics: Interference, Diffraction and Polarization [2]
- Coherent sources and the conditions for interference; Young's double-slit experiment
- Diffraction at a single slit and at a grating; resolving power of optical instruments
- Polarization and Brewster's law
# AREA: Current Electricity and Magnetism (9)
## 14. Current Electricity and Thermoelectricity [3]
- Ohm's law and Joule's law; resistance, resistivity, emf, potential difference, energy and power
- Resistors in series and parallel; Kirchhoff's laws
- Wheatstone bridge, metre bridge and potentiometer; a galvanometer converted to an ammeter and a voltmeter
- Seebeck and Peltier effects; the thermocouple
## 15. Magnetic Field and Magnetic Properties of Materials [3]
- Magnetic field of a straight conductor, a circular coil and a long solenoid
- Force on a moving charge and on a current-carrying conductor; torque on a coil; Hall effect
- Dia-, para- and ferromagnetism: domains, relative permeability, susceptibility and hysteresis
## 16. Electromagnetic Induction and Alternating Current [3]
- Faraday's and Lenz's laws; the AC generator, the transformer and eddy currents
- Self and mutual inductance; energy stored in an inductor
- Peak and rms values; impedance, phase, power and Q-factor of LCR circuits
- Rectification with a diode bridge
# AREA: Electrostatics and Capacitors (4)
## 17. Electrostatics and Capacitors [4]
- Electric charge, electrostatic induction and Coulomb's law
- Electric field of point charges; electric potential and potential energy; Gauss's law
- Capacitors: the parallel-plate capacitor, combinations, energy density and the effect of a dielectric
# AREA: Modern Physics (12)
## 18. Electrons and Photons [3]
- Motion of an electron in electric and magnetic fields
- J. J. Thomson's experiment and Millikan's oil-drop experiment
- Photons and the photoelectric effect: laws and Einstein's equation
## 19. Atomic Physics and X-rays [2]
- Bohr's theory of the hydrogen atom: energy levels and spectral series
- de Broglie waves and the uncertainty principle
- X-rays: production, properties and uses; Bragg's law
## 20. Nuclear Physics and Radioactivity [3]
- The nucleus: charge, size, mass and density; mass defect, binding energy per nucleon and E = mc²
- Nuclear fission and fusion
- Radioactivity: alpha, beta and gamma rays; the decay law, half-life and mean life; carbon dating
- Medical uses of nuclear radiation and its health hazards
## 21. Semiconductors and Logic Gates [2]
- Energy bands in metals, semiconductors and insulators; intrinsic and extrinsic semiconductors
- The p–n junction diode, its biasing and its use as a rectifier
- Logic gates: AND, OR, NOT, NAND and NOR
## 22. Particle Physics and Recent Trends [2]
- Particles and antiparticles; leptons and quarks; the Higgs boson
- Nanotechnology
- The Big Bang theory and Hubble's law

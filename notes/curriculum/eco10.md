# AREA: Basic Concepts
## 1. Basic Concepts of Economics and Allocation of Resources (अर्थशास्त्रको आधारभूत अवधारणा र साधनको बाँडफाँट) [12]
- Economy: meaning, types (capitalist, socialist, mixed) and features
- Scarcity, choice and opportunity cost
- Allocation of resources and the three central problems of an economy
- Macroeconomic variables: national income, consumption, saving, investment, inflation, unemployment, balance of payments
- Circular flow of income in a two-sector economy
# AREA: Microeconomics
## 2. Microeconomics (व्यष्टि अर्थशास्त्र) [30]
- Revenue: total, average and marginal revenue and their curves under perfect competition and monopoly
- Demand and supply: law, determinants, elasticity; market equilibrium
- Cost: fixed, variable, total, average and marginal cost and their curves
- Production: factors of production, law of variable proportions, returns to scale
- Market structures: perfect competition, monopoly, monopolistic competition and oligopoly
- Price and output determination under perfect competition and monopoly
- Distribution: rent, wages, interest and profit; marginal productivity theory of distribution
# AREA: Macroeconomics
## 3. Macroeconomics (समष्टि अर्थशास्त्र) [12]
- Government finance: public revenue, public expenditure, public debt and the budget
- Direct and indirect tax; the budget surplus and deficit; fiscal policy
- International trade: absolute and comparative advantage, free trade and protection, terms of trade
- Balance of trade and balance of payments; exchange rate; trade policy of Nepal
# AREA: Development Economics
## 4. Development Economics (विकास अर्थशास्त्र) [8]
- Economic growth and economic development; indicators of development
- Theories of economic development: classical, Marxian, Schumpeter, Rostow's stages, balanced and unbalanced growth
- Capital formation, population and development; human development index
- Bases of a socialist-oriented economy; features and problems of the Nepali economy
# AREA: Nepal's Economy
## 5. Nepal's Economy (नेपालको अर्थतन्त्र) [15]
- Agriculture: role, features, problems and prospects; land reform; irrigation, seeds and market
- Industry: role, types, problems and prospects; small and cottage industries; industrial policy
- Tourism: role, features, problems and prospects; tourism development in Nepal
- Foreign employment: role, remittance, problems and prospects
# AREA: Quantitative Methods
## 6. Quantitative Methods in Economics (अर्थशास्त्रमा परिमाणात्मक पद्धति) [19]
- Statistics: meaning, importance and limitations; collection of data; primary and secondary data
- Presentation of data: tables, frequency distribution and series
- Diagrammatic presentation: bar diagrams, pie chart, line graph
- Graphs of frequency distributions: histogram, frequency polygon, frequency curve and ogive
- Measures of central tendency: arithmetic mean (individual, discrete and continuous series)
- Median and quartiles
- Mode, including the empirical relation between mean, median and mode

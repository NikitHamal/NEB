# SUBJECT: Chemistry | TRACK: IOE (BE/BArch) | QUESTIONS: 22 | SYLLABUS: IOE Detailed Syllabus of B.E./B.Arch. Entrance Examination-2083 (Chemistry, full marks 30)
# AREA: Chemistry (22)
## 1. Physical Chemistry [9]
- Chemical arithmetic: Dalton's theory, laws of stoichiometry, atomic and molecular mass, empirical and molecular formula, limiting reactant, Avogadro's hypothesis, equivalent masses
- States of matter: gaseous, liquid and solid states
- Atomic structure and periodic classification of elements
- Oxidation, reduction and equilibrium
- Volumetric analysis
- Ionic equilibrium, acid, base and salt
- Electrochemistry
- Energetics of chemical reactions, chemical kinetics, chemical bonding and shape of molecules
## 2. Inorganic Chemistry [6]
- Non-metals: hydrogen, oxygen, ozone, water, nitrogen and its compounds, halogens, carbon, phosphorus, sulphur, noble gases and environmental pollution
- Metals: metallurgical principles, alkali metals, alkaline earth metals, coinage metals (copper, silver, gold)
- Extraction of metals: zinc, mercury, and iron compounds
## 3. Organic Chemistry [7]
- Introduction: fundamental principles, purification, nomenclature, structural isomerism, idea of reaction mechanism
- Hydrocarbons: alkanes, alkenes, alkynes, aromatic hydrocarbons
- Haloalkanes and haloarenes
- Alcohols, phenols and ethers
- Aldehydes, ketones, carboxylic acids and derivatives
- Nitro compounds and amines, aromatic and aliphatic

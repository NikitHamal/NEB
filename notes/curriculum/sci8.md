# AREA: Science and Technology
## 1. Scientific Learning
- science and the scientific method
- laboratory work and safety: hazard signs and precautions
- experimental work, recording observations and drawing conclusions
- research work: steps and the research report
- survey: questionnaire, sampling and tabulation
- measurement and units; project work
## 2. Information and Communication Technology
- tools of information and communication technology
- search engine, website and ISP
- social networking sites: uses and risks
- cybercrime, computer code of conduct, cyber law and internet security
- robotics and virtual reality
- artificial intelligence and cloud computing
## 3. Living Beings and Their Structure
- the cell: plant and animal cells, cell organelles and their functions
- interrelationship among cell, tissue, organ and organ system in the human body
- classification of living beings: five kingdoms and the vertebrate classes
- effects of microorganisms: useful microorganisms and disease-causing microorganisms
- protection of food from microorganisms; vaccination
## 4. Biodiversity and Environment
- biodiversity: meaning, levels and importance
- threats to biodiversity and its conservation
- ecosystem, food chain and food web
- sustainable development: meaning, principles and practices
- environment protection and local initiatives
## 5. Life Process
- reproduction: asexual and sexual
- sexual reproduction in plants: flower, pollination and fertilisation
- sexual reproduction in animals: internal and external fertilisation
- the seed: structure, germination and dispersal
- growth, nutrition and respiration in living beings
## 6. Force and Motion
- motion: distance, displacement, speed, velocity and acceleration
- distance-time and velocity-time graphs
- force and its effects; balanced and unbalanced forces
- Newton's laws of motion in everyday examples
- lever: classes, principle of moments and mechanical advantage
- pressure in solids, liquids and gases; atmospheric pressure and its uses
- Archimedes' principle, upthrust, floatation and density
## 7. Energy in Daily Life
- heat and temperature; thermometers and their scales
- thermal expansion of solids, liquids and gases
- transfer of heat: conduction, convection and radiation
- specific heat capacity and its daily applications
- light: reflection and refraction
- lenses and images; dispersion of light; the human eye and optical instruments
- energy sources, transformation and conservation
## 8. Electricity and Magnetism
- magnets, magnetic substances and the magnetic field
- terrestrial magnetism and the magnetic compass
- static electricity and electric charge
- electric circuit, current, potential difference and resistance
- Ohm's law and its verification
- household electrification: wiring, fuse, earthing and safety
- electric meter, unit (kWh) and the electricity bill
- magnetic effect of current; electric bell
## 9. Matter
- states of matter and the kinetic idea of particles
- element, compound and mixture; atom and molecule
- physical and chemical change
- solution, solute, solvent; saturated and unsaturated solution
- separation of mixtures: filtration, distillation, sublimation, chromatography
- acids, bases and salts; indicators and pH in daily life
- metals and non-metals; alloys
## 10. Materials Used in Daily Life
- water: sources, hardness, purification and water-borne disease
- water pollution and its control
- cement, glass and ceramics
- plastics and polymers: uses and environmental harm
- soap, detergent and fertiliser
- fibre: natural and synthetic
- fuel and combustion; fire safety
## 11. The Earth and Universe
- minerals: metallic and non-metallic, and their uses
- rocks and the structure of the earth
- origin and age of the earth
- the solar system: planets, satellites, asteroids and comets
- stars, constellations and galaxies
- space exploration and artificial satellites; Nepal in space science

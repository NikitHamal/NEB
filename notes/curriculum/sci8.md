# SUBJECT: Science and Technology | GRADE: 8 | CODE: Basic Education
# EXAM: Grade 8 assessment formats are set locally; follow the current specification issued by the school or local government.
# NOTE: Unit titles follow the CDC Grade 8 Science and Technology textbook (2080 BS). The bullets below are an authoring map, not a verbatim national exam grid.
# PART: Units
## 1. Scientific Learning [R]
- Science, observation, questions and testable hypotheses
- Fair investigations, variables, measurement, units, tables and graphs
- Laboratory safety, evidence, conclusions and project reporting
## 2. Information and Communication Technology [R]
- Digital devices, data, information, hardware and software
- Input, processing, storage and output; files and networks
- Internet use, search, privacy, security and responsible communication
## 3. Living Beings and Their Structure [R]
- Cells, tissues, organs and organ systems
- Plant and animal structures and their functions
- Microscopes, classification and adaptations
## 4. Biodiversity and Environment [R]
- Variety of life and its importance
- Habitats, food chains, ecosystems and balance
- Threats, conservation and local environmental action
## 5. Life Process [R]
- Nutrition, respiration, transport and excretion
- Growth, reproduction and response to surroundings
- Healthy habits and life processes in plants and animals
## 6. Force and Motion [R]
- Distance, displacement, speed and simple motion graphs
- Forces, friction, gravity and balanced forces
- Safe applications in transport and everyday tools
## 7. Energy in Daily Life [R]
- Forms, transfer and transformation of energy
- Renewable and non-renewable sources
- Efficient use and conservation in homes and communities
## 8. Electricity and Magnetism [R]
- Electric charge, cells, circuits and conductors
- Current, simple circuit diagrams and electrical safety
- Magnets, magnetic effects and useful devices
## 9. Matter [R]
- States and particle model of matter
- Elements, compounds and mixtures
- Physical and chemical changes; separation methods
## 10. Materials Used in Daily Life [R]
- Properties and uses of common natural and manufactured materials
- Metals, non-metals, plastics, fibres, fuels and household substances
- Safe use, reuse, recycling and environmental impact
## 11. The Earth and Universe [R]
- Earth systems, weather, seasons and the solar system
- The Moon, planets, stars and observations of the sky
- Natural resources, hazards and responsible stewardship

# AREA: Basic Components of Life (2)
## 1. Biomolecules: Carbohydrates, Lipids, Proteins, Enzymes and Minerals [2]
- Structure, types and biological role of carbohydrates and lipids
- Minerals and their biological roles
- Structure, types and biological role of proteins and enzymes
# AREA: Biodiversity (9)
## 2. Classification, Monera and Viruses [2]
- Two-kingdom system, taxonomic hierarchy and binomial nomenclature; five-kingdom and three-domain systems
- Bacteria: cell structure, types, nutrition and growth; general characteristics of cyanobacteria
- Viruses: characteristics, structure, chemical composition and types
## 3. Fungi, Lichens and Algae [2]
- Phycomycetes, Ascomycetes, Basidiomycetes and Deuteromycetes; structure and reproduction of yeast and Mucor
- Lichens: introduction and types
- Algae: Chlorophyceae, Rhodophyceae and Phaeophyceae; structure and reproduction of Spirogyra
## 4. Bryophytes, Pteridophytes and Gymnosperms [2]
- Liverworts, hornworts and mosses; structure and reproduction of Marchantia
- Pteridophytes; structure and reproduction of Dryopteris
- Gymnosperms; structure and reproduction of Pinus
## 5. Angiosperms and Economic Botany [3]
- Morphology of root, stem, leaf, inflorescence, flower and fruit
- Diagnostic characters, floral formulae and floral diagrams of Brassicaceae, Solanaceae, Fabaceae and Liliaceae
- Economic importance of viruses, bacteria, blue-green algae, fungi, algae, bryophytes, pteridophytes, gymnosperms and angiosperms of Nepal
- Medicinal plants of Nepal: neem, sarpagandha, yarsagumba, tulasi and ginger
# AREA: Ecology and Vegetation (4)
## 6. Ecology and Vegetation [4]
- Pond and forest ecosystems: structure and function; biotic interactions
- Carbon and nitrogen cycles; greenhouse effect, acid rain, ozone-layer depletion and climate change
- Forest types of Nepal; biological invasion
- Ecological succession: hydrosere and xerosere; ecological adaptation
# AREA: Cell Biology (5)
## 7. Cell Biology and Cell Division [5]
- Prokaryotic and eukaryotic cells; the cell theory
- Cell wall, cell membrane, mitochondria, chloroplasts, endoplasmic reticulum, Golgi body, lysosomes, ribosomes, nucleus, chromosomes, cilia, flagella and cell inclusions
- The cell cycle; amitosis, mitosis and meiosis and their significance
# AREA: Genetics (6)
## 8. Molecular Genetics: DNA, RNA and the Genetic Code [2]
- Composition, structure and functions of DNA and RNA
- DNA replication; the central dogma; the genetic code
## 9. Inheritance, Linkage, Mutation and Genetic Disorders [4]
- Mendelian terminology and laws; incomplete dominance and co-dominance
- Linkage (complete and incomplete) and crossing over
- Sex-linked inheritance: colour blindness in humans, eye colour in Drosophila
- Gene and chromosomal mutations; polyploidy
- Down's, Turner's, Edwards' and Klinefelter's syndromes; albinism; haemophilia
# AREA: Plant Anatomy (3)
## 10. Plant Anatomy [3]
- Plant tissues: characters, classification, structure and functions
- Types of vascular bundles
- T.S. of monocot and dicot root, stem and leaf
# AREA: Plant Physiology (6)
## 11. Water Relations of Plants [2]
- Diffusion, diffusion pressure deficit, osmosis and plasmolysis
- Osmotic pressure and potential, water potential, wall pressure and turgor pressure
- Absorption, imbibition, ascent of sap, transpiration, guttation and wilting
## 12. Photosynthesis and Respiration [3]
- Photosynthetic pigments; photosystems I and II; the light reactions
- Calvin cycle (C3), Hatch–Slack pathway (C4) and photorespiration; factors; bacterial photosynthesis
- Aerobic and anaerobic respiration: glycolysis, oxidative decarboxylation, Krebs cycle, electron transport and oxidative phosphorylation; factors
## 13. Plant Growth, Germination and Dormancy [1]
- Auxins, gibberellins and cytokinins: roles and applications
- Seed germination and its types; seed dormancy
# AREA: Developmental and Applied Botany (5)
## 14. Developmental Botany [2]
- Asexual reproduction
- Sporogenesis and gametogenesis in angiosperms
- Pollination and fertilization
- Monocot and dicot embryos; types and functions of endosperm
## 15. Applied Botany [3]
- Plant tissue culture: concept, types and applications
- Genetic engineering: concept and applications
- Biofertilizers, green manures, plant breeding, bio-engineering, food safety and security

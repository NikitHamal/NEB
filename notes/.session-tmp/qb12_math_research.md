Year-by-year header variants (exact):

- **2079** — `Sub.Code : 0081'K'` / "New course" / "For regular students (excluding technical stream's students)"
- **2080** — `Sub.Code : 0081'H'` / "(New course)" / "(For general stream)" / "(For the students whose first two digits of registration number starts from 78 or 79)"
- **2081** — `Sub.Code : 4021` / "(New Course)" / "(For General Stream Regular Students)" / "(For the regular students whose first two digits of registration number starts from 80)"
- **2082** — `Sub.Code : 0081 (New course) Set 'R'` / "{For the students whose first two digits of registration number starts from 80 (partial) and 81 (regular)}"

**Structure (constant across all four years and the CDC model paper):**

| | Count | Marks | Printed as |
|---|---|---|---|
| Group A (MCQ) | 11 (Q1–Q11) | 1 each | `11x1=11` |
| Group B (short) | 8 (Q12–Q19) | 5 each | `8x5=40` |
| Group C (long) | 3 (Q20–Q22) | 8 each | `3x8=24` |
| Total | 22 | 75 | Time 3 hrs |

**"Or" choices — exactly two per paper, always in the same two slots:** Q11 (last MCQ of Group A) and Q19 (last question of Group B). **Group C never has an "Or".** Everything else is compulsory ("Attempt all the questions.").

**Sub-parts:** Yes. Group B and Group C questions carry labelled sub-parts `a) b) c)` (Group C is almost always exactly a/b/c summing to 8; several Group B questions run to `a)…e)` five 1-mark parts, and 2079 Q19 / 2080 Q16 use roman `i) … v)`). Per-sub-part marks are printed in the right margin, e.g. `[3]`, `[2]`, `(1+4)`, `3+2`.

**Stem-with-context style is real:** several Group B items are a single stimulus (a figure, a table, a stated theorem) followed by 5 one-mark probes — e.g. 2080 Q16 is a figure of f(x) on [a,b] with five 1-mark questions; 2082 Q12 is five unrelated 1-mark recall prompts.

**How the optional unit is offered — this varies by year and is NOT uniform:**

- **2081 and 2082 (and the CDC model question 2080):** clean Computational Methods **or** Mechanics pairing. Q11 MCQ has an "Or" (elimination/Cramer-style item vs a projectile item) and Q19 has an "Or" (simplex LPP vs a mechanics problem). This matches the grid exactly: 1 MCQ + 1 SAQ = 6 marks.
- **2079 and 2080:** different. **Q18 is a compulsory simplex LPP question for everyone** (Computational Methods), and the "Or" at Q11/Q19 pairs **Mechanics against an economics/input-output (Leontief) alternative**:
  - 2079 Q11: momentum change MCQ **Or** "In an input-output model if A=input coefficient of matrix, I be unit matrix then each diagonal elements of the (I-A) is positive and [I-A] is positive then the system is called A) viable B) unviable C) unit elastic D) elastic"
  - 2079 Q19: projectile (5 one-mark parts) **Or** "Given is the transition matrix." (Agriculture/Industry input-output table) a) input coefficient matrix b) total output
  - 2080 Q11: "What is the amount of gravity exerted by the earth on the mass of 100kg?" **Or** "What is the quadratic equation of equilibrium for the demand equation 2x+y-10=0 and supply equation y²-8x-4=0?"
  - 2080 Q19: like parallel forces **Or** a two-industry Leontief input-output table ("Determine the output vector of the economy, if final demand at A is increased by 10% and the final demand of B is decreased by 10%.")

  **Flagged discrepancy:** I downloaded and text-searched the official CDC curriculum PDF (Secondary Curriculum 2078, Grade 11-12 Mathematics, Mat. 401/402). Grade 12 area 7 is **only** "Computational methods" (Gauss Elimination, Gauss Seidel up to 3 variables; simplex LPP) **or Mechanics** (triangle law / Lami's theorem; Newton's laws and projectile). The words "input", "output vector", "economics", "Leontief", "demand" do **not** appear anywhere in the Grade 12 content. So the 2079/2080 economics alternatives were off the 2078 curriculum as published. I am reporting what is printed; I could not find a document explaining it.

**2082 change — verbatim from the paper:**

```
                        Group 'A'
```

MCQs are now a separate booklet issued 30 minutes into the exam (I have both booklets: the main paper `0081(New course) set 'R'` pages 3–5, and the MCQ booklet pages 1–2). Group A is otherwise unchanged (11 items, 1 mark each, one "Or" at Q11).

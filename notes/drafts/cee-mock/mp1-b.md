**83.** (b) — Fe: [Ar] 3d⁶ 4s². By Hund's rule the six 3d electrons fill five orbitals singly first, leaving 4 unpaired.
**84.** (d) — Balmer lines end on n = 2 (visible region); jumps to n = 1 are Lyman and to n = 3 are Paschen.
**85.** (a) — N has a stable half-filled 2p³ set, so its IE (1402 kJ mol⁻¹) exceeds that of O (1314), where the paired 2p electron is easier to remove.

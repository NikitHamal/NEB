**82.** (a) — 2H₂ + O₂ → 2H₂O: 2 mol H₂ needs 1 mol O₂ but only 0.5 mol is present, so O₂ limits and gives 1 mol (18 g) water.
**83.** (d) — λ = h/p = h/√(2mK), so λ ∝ 1/√K: four times K halves λ.
**84.** (c) — one orbital each of 3s, 3p and 3d has mₗ = 0; three orbitals hold 6 electrons.
**85.** (c) — chlorine; fluorine's small, crowded 2p shell repels the incoming electron, so F is less negative than Cl.
**86.** (a) — the central I has 2 bond pairs and 3 lone pairs (sp³d); the lone pairs occupy equatorial positions, so the ion is linear.
**87.** (b) — the central carbon forms two σ bonds and two π bonds (to two different carbons), so it is sp-hybridized and linear.
**88.** (d) — disproportionation: Cl goes from 0 to −1 in NaCl (reduction) and from 0 to +5 in NaClO₃ (oxidation).

**92.** (b) — [OH⁻] = 2 × 0.001 = 0.002 M; pOH = 2.70, so pH = 14 − 2.70 = 11.3.

**97.** (d) — α count = (238 − 206)/4 = 8, lowering Z to 92 − 16 = 76; six β⁻ raise it to 82.

**108.** (b) — number the five-carbon chain from the end nearer the OH: OH on C-2, methyl on C-4.

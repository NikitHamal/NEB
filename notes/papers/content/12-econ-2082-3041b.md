### Group A

@s 1
Two causes of the problem of choice: (i) human wants are unlimited, and (ii) the resources that satisfy them (land, labour, capital, time) are scarce and have alternative uses. Scarcity forces every society to choose.

@s 2
Marginal cost of the 8th unit = TC₈ − TC₇ = 727 − 624 = **Rs. 103**.

@s 3
The **central bank** (in Nepal, Nepal Rastra Bank) is the lender of last resort: it lends to commercial banks when no one else will, to save them from failure.

@s 4
The money market deals in short-term funds (up to one year, e.g. treasury bills), whereas the capital market deals in long-term funds (above one year, e.g. shares and debentures).

@s 5
Two indicators of human development: (i) **life expectancy at birth** (health) and (ii) **education** (mean and expected years of schooling). Per-capita income (GNI) is the third indicator of the Human Development Index.

@s 6
The **World Trade Organization (WTO)**, established in 1995, which replaced the GATT.

@s 7
Two objectives of the Sixteenth Plan (2081/82 to 2085/86), stated in general terms: (i) to achieve high, sustainable and inclusive economic growth with productive employment, and (ii) to build quality infrastructure and human capital so that Nepal can graduate from the least developed country group with a resilient economy.

@s 8
**Sustainable Development Goal 6** (Clean Water and Sanitation) ensures availability and sustainable management of water and sanitation for all.

@s 9
In the singular sense, statistics means a numerical measure computed from a sample or a set of observations, such as the mean, the standard deviation or the correlation coefficient. (The plural sense means the collection of numerical data.)

@s 10
Primary data are collected first-hand by the researcher for the specific purpose of the study, so they are more accurate, reliable and up to date for that purpose than secondary data collected by others for some other purpose.

@s 11
Coefficient of range = (L − S) ÷ (L + S) = 25 ÷ 85 = **0.294**. The value is small (about 29 per cent), which means the dispersion of the series is low: the items are fairly close together and the series is fairly uniform.

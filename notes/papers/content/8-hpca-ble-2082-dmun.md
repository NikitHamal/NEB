### Group 'A' — Very Short Answers

@s 1
A **nerve cell (neuron)** is the basic structural and functional unit of the nervous system that receives and transmits electrical and chemical nerve impulses between the brain, spinal cord and other parts of the body. [[1]]

@s 2
The **reproductive system** is the biological system of primary and secondary sex organs in an organism that work together to produce gametes, facilitate fertilization and produce offspring for the continuation of the species. [[1]]

@s 3
Any two characteristics of a healthy person: [[1]]
1. **Physical fitness and energy:** Has a disease-free body, normal appetite, sound sleep and stamina to perform daily tasks.
2. **Mental and social well-being:** Remains emotionally calm, positive and self-confident, and maintains harmonious relationships in society.

@s 4
Anemia is caused by the deficiency of **haemoglobin** (or **red blood cells / iron**) in the blood. [[1]]

@s 5
**Junk food** refers to pre-packaged, ultra-processed or fast foods (such as instant noodles, chips and sugary soft drinks) that contain high amounts of refined sugar, salt and unhealthy fats but very little protein, vitamins, minerals or dietary fibre. [[1]]

@s 6
**Unsafe abortion** is a procedure for terminating an unintended pregnancy carried out either by individuals lacking the required medical training or in an unhygienic environment that does not meet minimum medical standards. [[1]]

@s 7
There are **7 court players** in a Kabaddi team on the playing field at a time (out of a total squad of 12 players including 5 substitutes). [[1]]

@s 8
For standard international matches, a football field has a **length of $100\text{ m}$ to $110\text{ m}$** (general FIFA range $90\text{ m}$ to $120\text{ m}$) and a **width of $64\text{ m}$ to $75\text{ m}$** (general FIFA range $45\text{ m}$ to $90\text{ m}$). [[1]]

@s 9
In creative arts, **construction work** (*nirman karya*) is the activity of making three-dimensional models, toys or decorative items by cutting, folding, carving or assembling materials such as paper, cardboard, clay, wood, wire or locally available waste materials. [[1]]

@s 10
**Papier collé** (French for *"pasted paper"*) is a visual art technique and form of collage in which cut or torn pieces of coloured, patterned or printed paper are glued onto a flat surface to create an artistic design or picture. [[1]]

### Group 'B' — Short Answers

@s 11
Two differences between **ovary** and **testes**: [[2]]

| S.N. | Ovary | Testes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | Ovaries are the primary **female** reproductive organs located inside the lower abdominal (pelvic) cavity. | Testes are the primary **male** reproductive organs located outside the abdominal cavity inside the scrotal sac. |
| 2. | They produce female gametes (**ova / eggs**) and secrete female sex hormones (**oestrogen and progesterone**). | They produce male gametes (**spermatozoa**) and secrete the male sex hormone (**testosterone**). |

@s 12
Any two importances of a **school health screening test**: [[2]]
1. **Early detection of health defects:** Regular screening of vision, hearing, dental health, height and weight helps identify malnutrition, refractive errors or infections early so timely treatment can be given.
2. **Improved learning and attendance:** Healthy students concentrate better in class, miss fewer school days and develop lifelong habits of personal hygiene.

@s 13
Points to consider for preserving nutrients while cooking food: [[2]]
1. **Wash vegetables before cutting, not after:** Washing chopped vegetables leaches out water-soluble vitamins (Vitamin B-complex and Vitamin C).
2. **Avoid over-cooking and use a covered pot with minimal water:** Cook food in just enough water with a lid on, and reuse leftover cooking water in soups or curries instead of discarding it.

@s 14
Suggestions to family members to prevent and avoid cancer: [[2]]
1. **Avoid tobacco, smoking and alcohol completely:** Chewing tobacco, smoking cigarettes and consuming alcohol are the leading preventable causes of lung, oral and liver cancers.
2. **Eat a fresh, fibre-rich diet and exercise regularly:** Consume plenty of green leafy vegetables, seasonal fruits and whole grains, avoid charred or heavily processed foods, protect skin from excessive UV radiation, and go for periodic medical check-ups.

@s 15
Hand and arm exercises are particularly suitable for **throwing, striking and racquet/court sports such as volleyball, basketball, shot put, javelin throw, cricket, badminton and gymnastics**. [[2]]
- **Reason:** These sports require strong shoulder, biceps, triceps and wrist muscles to serve, spike, pass, throw or swing with power and accuracy, and proper warm-up exercises of the arms prevent muscle sprains and joint dislocation.

@s 16
An athlete can minimize the risk of injuries while landing in the **long jump** by following these measures: [[2]]
1. **Bend the knees and hips upon landing in the sand pit:** Bending the knees absorbs the impact shock smoothly and shifts the body weight forward rather than jarring the ankles or spine.
2. **Ensure the landing pit is filled with soft, levelled, stone-free wet sand** and wear proper athletic shoes after adequate warm-up and stretching.

@s 17
In **Bhastrika Pranayama** (bellows breath; as well as rapid active exhalation in *Kapalbhati*), inhalation and exhalation are performed more rapidly and forcefully than in normal respiration. [[2]]
- **Two advantages:**
  1. It strengthens the lungs, clears excess phlegm from the respiratory passages and increases oxygen supply to the blood.
  2. It stimulates digestion and metabolism while energizing the nervous system and improving mental alertness.

@s 18
In volleyball, **service** is the skill of putting the ball into play by the right-back player (*server*) standing behind the end line (within the $9\text{ m}$ service zone), hitting the ball with one hand or arm so that it passes cleanly over the net into the opponent's court. Common types of service include the **underhand service** (easiest for beginners), **overhead (tennis) service** and **jump service**. [[2]]

@s 19
Two differences between **Kaharwa Taal** and **Rupak Taal**: [[2]]

| S.N. | Kaharwa Taal | Rupak Taal |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. | It consists of **8 matras (beats)** divided into **2 equal vibhags** of $4 + 4$ beats (*Dha Ge Na Ti $\mid$ Na Ka Dhi Na*). | It consists of **7 matras (beats)** divided into **3 vibhags** of $3 + 2 + 2$ beats (*Tin Tin Na $\mid$ Dhin Na $\mid$ Dhin Na*). |
| 2. | It has **1 Tali** (on beat 1, *Sam*) and **1 Khali** (on beat 5), and is widely used in folk, light and bhajan songs. | In classical tradition its first beat starts with a wave (*Khali/Sam* on beat 1) and **Tali** on beats 4 and 6, used in semi-classical and devotional music. |

@s 20
**Sorathi dance** is a traditional folk dance drama predominant among the **Magar and Gurung** communities (and also performed by Chhetri and Thakuri communities) in the hilly region of western Nepal, especially in Gandaki and Lumbini provinces. Based on the ancient legend of King Jaisingh and Queen Sorathi, men and women dressed in traditional attire dance in a circle to the rhythm of the **Madal**, accompanied by narrative songs and *Maruni* dancers during festivals between Dashain and Tihar. [[2]]

### Group 'C' — Long Answers

@s 21
Four major **problems of community health** in Nepal are: [[4]]
1. **Environmental pollution and improper waste management**
2. **Lack of safe drinking water and sanitation**
3. **Malnutrition and food adulteration**
4. **Spread of communicable diseases and rapid population growth**

**Explanation of any two:**
- **1. Environmental pollution and improper waste management:** Unplanned urbanization, open dumping of household garbage, plastic waste and untreated sewage pollute air, soil and water sources, creating breeding grounds for flies and mosquitoes that spread cholera, typhoid and dengue.
- **2. Lack of safe drinking water and sanitation:** Many rural and peri-urban communities still rely on contaminated surface water or unboiled water and lack proper toilets, leading to frequent outbreaks of water-borne diseases such as diarrhoea, dysentery and jaundice.

@s 22
**"Heart disease can be controlled by a healthy lifestyle."** This statement is completely true because most cardiovascular diseases (such as hypertension, coronary artery blockage and heart attack) are non-communicable lifestyle disorders. The statement is supported by the following arguments: [[4]]
1. **Balanced and heart-friendly diet:** Eating fresh vegetables, fruits, whole grains and pulses while minimizing saturated fats, trans fats, excess salt and refined sugar prevents cholesterol plaque build-up (*atherosclerosis*) in the coronary arteries.
2. **Regular physical exercise and weight control:** At least $30$ to $45$ minutes of daily brisk walking, yoga, cycling or sports strengthens the cardiac muscle, improves blood circulation and prevents obesity and diabetes.
3. **Abstinence from smoking and alcohol:** Smoking damages blood vessel linings and raises blood pressure, while excess alcohol weakens the heart muscle; avoiding both drastically lowers the risk of heart attack.
4. **Stress management and adequate sleep:** Practising meditation, pranayama and getting $7$ to $8$ hours of restful sleep keeps blood pressure and stress hormones under control, protecting long-term heart health.

@s 23
**First aid** is the immediate, temporary medical care and assistance given to an injured or suddenly ill person at the site of the accident using available resources before professional medical treatment arrives. Its main aims are to preserve life, prevent the condition from worsening and promote recovery. [[4]]

**List of essential materials used in a First Aid Box:**
1. Sterile cotton wool and gauze pads
2. Roller bandages, crêpe bandage and triangular bandage
3. Adhesive plaster / band-aids and medical tape
4. Antiseptic lotion or cream (such as Dettol, Savlon or Povidone-iodine)
5. A pair of clean scissors, tweezers and safety pins
6. Oral Rehydration Salt (ORS / *Jeevan Jal*) packets
7. Digital thermometer and disposable medical gloves
8. Basic over-the-counter medicines (paracetamol, antacid tablets and burn ointment)

@s 24
Any four fundamental **rules of the volleyball game**: [[4]]
1. **Team composition and court rotation:** Each team has **6 players** on the court ($3$ front-row and $3$ back-row players). When the receiving team wins the right to serve, its players must rotate **one position clockwise**.
2. **Maximum three hits per side:** A team is allowed a maximum of **three touches** (in addition to a legal block contact) to return the ball over the net into the opponent's court, and no single player may hit the ball twice consecutively.
3. **Service rules:** The server must hit the ball with one hand from behind the end line within **8 seconds** after the referee's whistle, without stepping on or inside the end line (*foot fault*) before contact.
4. **Net and boundary faults:** Players must not touch the net during play; a ball landing on the boundary line is counted as **"in"**, whereas a ball landing outside the court, passing under the net or touching an antenna is counted as **"out"**.

@s 25
**"Nepali art has gained fame in the international arena."** This statement is justified by the rich, centuries-old artistic heritage of Nepal that is admired worldwide: [[4]]
1. **Global contributions of Araniko:** In the 13th century, the great Nepali architect and artist **Araniko** led a troop of 80 artisans to Tibet and China, where he built the famous **White Pagoda (*Miaoying Temple*) in Beijing** and popularized pagoda architecture across East Asia.
2. **World-famous Pagoda and Stupa architecture:** Seven monument zones of the **Kathmandu Valley** (including Pashupatinath, Swayambhunath, Boudhanath and the Durbar Squares of Kathmandu, Patan and Bhaktapur) and **Lumbini** are listed as **UNESCO World Heritage Sites** for their unmatched brick, stone and timber architecture.
3. **Paubha / Thangka painting and Mithila art:** Traditional Newa **Paubha** paintings, Tibetan-Buddhist **Thangkas** and vibrant **Mithila (Madhubani) folk paintings** from Janakpur are exhibited in major international museums and art galleries across Europe, America and Asia.
4. **Intricate woodcarving and metal sculpture:** Nepali *Tundals* (carved wooden struts), *Ankhijhyal* (peacock and lattice windows) and lost-wax (*cire perdue*) bronze and copper-gilt statues crafted in Patan are prized globally as masterpieces of traditional craftsmanship.

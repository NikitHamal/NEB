### Reading and Comprehension Solutions

@s 1
**A. True or False** $(5\times 1=5)$ [[5]]

| Item | Statement | Answer | Evidence from the letter |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- |
| (a) | The students are happy with the establishment of the computer lab. | **True** | Paragraph 1 states: *"We are overjoyed seeing a computer lab at our school."* |
| (b) | The current computer lab has all the necessary equipment. | **False** | Paragraph 2 states: *"In fact, the lab is lacking some necessary equipment."* |
| (c) | The students have access to the internet in the computer lab. | **False** | Paragraph 2 states: *"Third, our lab does not have an internet connection."* |
| (d) | The students can learn about anything in the computer lab. | **False** | Because only a few computers have the required software installed (*"This limits our learning"*) and there is no internet connection (*"we cannot access the world of knowledge and information"*), their learning is restricted. |
| (e) | Students are planning an improvement in facilities themselves. | **False** | The students are requesting the Head Teacher to provide the facilities (*"We would be thankful if you provided us with all the necessities for a modern computer lab at our school"*), not funding or installing them on their own. |

*(Note: On the scanned exam sheet, a candidate had pencilled "T" next to (d) and (e); the letter text directly contradicts both statements, so the correct answers for (d) and (e) are **False**.)*

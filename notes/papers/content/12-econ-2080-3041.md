### Group A

@s 1
**Allocation of resources** is the process of distributing scarce resources among their competing uses, that is, deciding what to produce, how to produce and for whom to produce, so that human wants are satisfied as fully as possible.

@s 2
Profit = TR - TC = 5,000 - 4,200 = **Rs. 800**.

@s 3
A **progressive tax** is a tax whose rate rises as the taxable income (or base) rises, so that the rich pay a higher proportion of income than the poor. Income tax with slab rates is an example.

@s 4
Two arguments for free trade: (i) it permits international **specialisation** according to comparative advantage, which raises efficiency and world output; (ii) it gives consumers a **wider choice of goods at lower prices**, and competition from imports improves quality.

@s 5
The **Human Development Index (HDI)** is a composite index published by the UNDP that measures the average achievement of a country in three dimensions: a long and healthy life (life expectancy), knowledge (years of schooling) and a decent standard of living (GNI per capita). Its value lies between 0 and 1.

@s 6
**SAFTA** is the free trade agreement among the member countries of SAARC, signed in 2004 and in force from 1 January 2006, which aims to reduce tariffs on trade among the South Asian countries step by step to promote regional trade.

@s 7
Two importance of remittance: (i) it **raises household income and reduces poverty** and finances education, health and consumption; (ii) it earns **foreign exchange**, which pays for imports and strengthens the balance of payments.

@s 8
**Goal 14, Life below water** (conservation and sustainable use of oceans, seas and marine resources), because Nepal is a landlocked country with no sea.

@s 9
Two importance of statistics in economics: (i) it **presents complex economic data in a simple form** (tables, averages, diagrams) for understanding; (ii) it helps in **economic planning and policy making** and in testing economic theories with facts.

@s 10
**Sampling** studies only a small, representative part of the population, so it needs far **less money, time and manpower** than a census, which has to enumerate every unit.

@s 11
Mean = (10 + 20 + X + 40 + 60) / 5 = 35, so 130 + X = 175 and **X = 45**.

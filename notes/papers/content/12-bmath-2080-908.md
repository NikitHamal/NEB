## Group 'A'
<small>Short questions · [10×2×3=60]</small>

@q 1
(a) Rewrite $|2x-5|\le3$ without absolute value sign. [[3]]
(b) Find the domain and the range of the following relation $R=\{(1,1),(2,4),(3,9),(4,16)\}$. [[3]]

@q 2
(a) A parent places in the saving bank Rs. 500 on his child's first birth, Rs. 1000 on his second, Rs. 1500 on his third and so on increasing the amount by Rs. 500 on each birthday. How much will he save up when the child reaches the sixteenth birthday, the latter inclusive ? [[3]]
(b) Insert 4 geometric means between $\dfrac23$ and $5\dfrac1{16}$. [[3]]

@q 3
(a) How many license plates consisting of 3 different numbers of digits can be made out of given digits 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6 ? [[3]]
(b) If $A=\begin{pmatrix}-3&-2\\5&3\end{pmatrix}$ and $B=\begin{pmatrix}3&2\\-5&3\end{pmatrix}$, find $|AB|$. [[3]]

@q 4
(a) The middle point of a line is $(2,3)$ and one end of the same line is $(3,4)$. Find the co-ordinates of the other end. [[3]]
(b) Find the equation of a straight line making 9 and 15 as x-intercept and y-intercept respectively. [[3]]

@q 5
(a) Using log-table, evaluate $\dfrac{(3.678)^4}{\sqrt[3]{42.75}}$. [[3]]
(b) Evaluate: $\displaystyle\lim_{x\to3}\dfrac{x^3-27}{x^2-5x+6}$. [[3]]

@q 6
(a) Examine the continuity or discontinuity of the function $f(x)=\dfrac{x^2-16}{x-4}$ at $x=4$. [[3]]
(b) Find $\dfrac{dy}{dx}$ if $x=2a(t+3)$ and $y=a(t^2+7)$. [[3]]

@q 7
(a) Evaluate: $\displaystyle\int\dfrac{x^2-4}{x+2}\,dx$. [[3]]
(b) If for 2 units of production, total revenue is Rs. 200, find the total revenue function given that $MR=10+20x-3x^2$. [[3]]

@q 8
(a) Find the standard deviation from the following data: 158, 166, 164, 160, 172, 170. [[3]]
(b) For two events A and B of $P(A)=0.40$, $P(B)=0.75$ and $P(A\cap B)=0.61$, find $P(A\cup B)$. [[3]]

@q 9
(a) A contractor has to complete the work of a road in 16 days. He employed 30 persons for 12 days and completes $\dfrac57$ of the work. How many more persons should be employed now in order to complete the work in time ? [[3]]
(b) Mohan bought 100 kg of oranges at Rs. 72 per kg. If 10 kg orange are rotten and are of no use to sell, at what rate must the remaining oranges be sold so as to gain 15% on the whole outlay ? [[3]]

@q 10
(a) If 90 dollars = 75 Euro, 60 Euro = 89.10 Swiss francs, 56 Swiss francs = \$25 and \$1 = Rs. 134.40, how many rupee are equivalent to 500 dollars ? [[3]]
(b) Find the amount of an immediate annuity of Rs. 700 a year for 4 years at 5 percent per annum. [[3]]

## Group 'B'
<small>Short answer questions · [8×5=40]</small>

@q 11
Prove that $\begin{vmatrix}1+a&1&1\\1&1+a&1\\1&1&1+a\end{vmatrix}=a^2(a+3)$. [[5]]

@q 12
Find from first principles, the derivative of $\dfrac{1}{1+x}$. [[5]]

@q 13
If the revenue function is $R=Q-3Q^2$ and cost function $C=Q^2-2Q$, find the value of maximum profit. [[5]]

@q 14
Find the maximum value of the objective function $G=8x+3y$ subject to $-x+y\le6$, $3x+y\ge6$, $x\le2$. [[5]]

@q 15
Calculate the Mean Deviation from the mean of the following data: [[5]]

| Bonus | 10 | 15 | 20 | 25 | 30 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Frequency | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 | 3 |

@q 16
A started a business with a capital of Rs. 7000 in the beginning of the year. After 4 months, he admitted B with a capital of Rs. 6000; after 6 months they admitted C with a capital of Rs. 8000. If the profit at the end of the year amount to Rs. 15000, find their profits individually. [[5]]

@q 17
A bill drawn for Rs. 2700 was discounted in the bank for Rs. 2655. If the rate of discount was 5% p.a., find the time for which the bill was drawn. [[5]]

@q 18
The simple interest on Rs. 3640 for 3 years at a certain rate percent p.a. is Rs. 273. Find the compound interest of the same sum for the same time at the same rate of interest. [[5]]

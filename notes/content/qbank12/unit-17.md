The Compulsory English paper has no multiple-choice group, but it does carry **15 marks of short objective items**: Question 7 is ten grammar items of one mark each, and Question 8 is five vocabulary items of one mark each. These are the cheapest marks in the paper, because each item tests one rule and the rules repeat. This chapter gives ten items for each grammar unit of the Grade 12 book (units 21 to 30) and a vocabulary bank for unit 35, every item with its key. Cover the answer column, write your answer, then check.

::: key How the marks are built
Q7 Grammar: 10 items x 1 = **10 marks**. Q8 Vocabulary: 5 items x 1 = **5 marks**. A wrong item costs one mark and nothing more, so never leave one blank. The items below are original practice items written to the same type as the board papers; they are not copied from any paper.
:::

## 17.1 Question tags and questions

Fill each blank with the correct question tag.

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | She can swim, ____? | can't she |
| 2 | Let's go out, ____? | shall we |
| 3 | Nobody called, ____? | did they |
| 4 | I am late, ____? | aren't I |
| 5 | You have never been to Pokhara, ____? | have you |
| 6 | Open the door, ____? | will you |
| 7 | He hardly eats anything, ____? | does he |
| 8 | There is some milk left, ____? | isn't there |
| 9 | They had better leave now, ____? | hadn't they |
| 10 | She used to live here, ____? | didn't she |

**Rule to remember:** a positive statement takes a negative tag and a negative statement takes a positive tag. Words such as *nobody, never, hardly, seldom* make the statement negative, so the tag is positive.

## 17.4 Subject-verb agreement

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Neither of the boys ____ late. (be) | is |
| 2 | The news ____ shocking. (be) | is |
| 3 | Each of the girls ____ a prize. (have) | has |
| 4 | Ram and Shyam ____ friends. (be) | are |
| 5 | Either the teacher or the students ____ responsible. (be) | are |
| 6 | Mathematics ____ my favourite subject. (be) | is |

## Chapter summary

- Q7 and Q8 together are **15 marks of one-mark items**; attempt every one.
- Tags: opposite polarity; negative words give positive tags; *I am* gives *aren't I*; *let's* gives *shall we*.
- Modals: *must* is certainty or rule, *can't* is impossibility, *should have* is regret, *needn't* is no necessity.
- Agreement: nearer noun rule for *either...or*; *a number of* plural, *the number of* singular.
- Reported speech moves the tense one step back and changes *now, today, tomorrow* to *then, that day, the next day*.
- Conditionals: type 1 *will*, type 2 *would*, type 3 *would have*; *had I known* means *if I had known*.
- Passive: object becomes subject; use *be + past participle*, with the tense of the active verb kept.
- Vocabulary: learn the idioms in 17.11 and the commonly misspelled words.

**Instructions.** Computer-based paper pattern of the IOE B.E./B.Arch. entrance (2083 booklet): **100 questions, 140 marks, 2 hours (120 minutes).** Section A has 60 questions of 1 mark; Section B has 40 questions of 2 marks. Each question has four options and one correct answer. **A wrong answer costs 10 % of that question's marks**: $-0.1$ in Section A, $-0.2$ in Section B. Leave a question unanswered to lose nothing. The paper is numbered straight through and is arranged by subject within each section, as in the official model questions. Constants: $g=10$ m s⁻² unless stated, $h=6.63\times10^{-34}$ J s, $e=1.6\times10^{-19}$ C, $N_A=6.02\times10^{23}$.

**Score yourself:** score $=(\text{right}\times1+\text{right}_B\times2)-0.1\times\text{wrong}_A-0.2\times\text{wrong}_B$. Count from the answer key at the end.

| Subject | Section A (1 mark) | Section B (2 marks) | Questions | Marks |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Mathematics | 1–20 | 61–75 | 35 | 50 |
| Physics | 21–34 | 76–88 | 27 | 40 |
| Chemistry | 35–48 | 89–96 | 22 | 30 |

*The split of questions between subjects within the two sections is not published by IOE; this paper uses the 20+15 / 14+13 / 14+8 / 12+4 distribution that fits 60 + 40 questions and the subject marks 50 / 40 / 30 / 20.*

**Pacing.** 120 minutes for 100 questions is 72 seconds each on average. A sound plan: Section A (60 questions) in 45 minutes, Section B (40) in 60 minutes, 15 minutes for returning to skipped questions.

Sit this paper exactly as you will sit the real MECEE-BL: at a desk, with a watch,
an OMR-style answer sheet (or a plain sheet ruled 1–200), a pencil and this book
closed. Give yourself **3 hours (180 minutes)** for the **200 questions** — an
average of 54 seconds each — and do not pause the clock.

::: key Instructions, as the real paper gives them
- Every question carries **1 mark**. Four options; **one** is the best answer.
- A wrong answer costs **−0.25**; a blank costs **0**.
- **How to score yourself:** count the right answers R and the wrong answers W, then
  your score is **R − 0.25 W** out of 200. Compare it with the 50th-percentile habit:
  in recent cycles a Group I merit place began around 120–140 — set your personal
  target above that and track it across the five papers.
- Attempt strategy: sweep once through the questions you *know* (about 50 minutes a
  subject block), mark and return to the ones you can reason out, and never leave a
  question blank merely out of fear. From Chapter 1, a guess pays when your chance p
  of being right beats 0.2 (E = 1.25p − 0.25): killing two options earns +0.375 a
  guess, killing one +0.17, and even a blind four-way guess is mildly positive
  (+0.06) — but blind guessing swings the score, so reserve it for the last minutes.
:::

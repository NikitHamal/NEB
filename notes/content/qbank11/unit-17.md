The Grade 11 Compulsory English paper has no multiple-choice group, but it does carry **15 marks of short objective items**: Question 7 is ten grammar items of one mark each, and Question 8 is five vocabulary items of one mark each. These are the cheapest marks in the paper, because each item tests one rule and the rules repeat. This chapter gives ten practice items for each grammar unit of the Grade 11 book (units 21 to 30 of the English book) and a bank of vocabulary items for Question 8 (unit 35).

::: key How the marks are built
Q7 Grammar: 10 items x 1 = **10 marks**. Q8 Vocabulary: 5 items x 1 = **5 marks**. A wrong item costs one mark and nothing more, so never leave one blank. The items below are original practice items written to the type of the board papers; none is claimed to be from a particular year's paper.
:::

::: tip How to use the tables
Cover the **Answer** column and write your own answer. For every item you miss, do not just copy the answer: read the rule at the end of the section and say aloud why the wrong option is wrong. Ten minutes a day on one section is enough to finish the chapter in two weeks.
:::

## 17.1 Word classes, contracted forms and dictionary use

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The word class of *quickly* in "She quickly left." | adverb |
| 2 | The word class of *beautiful* in "a beautiful garden" | adjective |
| 3 | The word class of *under* in "under the table" | preposition |
| 4 | The word class of *but* in "I tried but failed." | conjunction |
| 5 | The word class of *Wow!* in "Wow! What a view!" | interjection |
| 6 | The plural of *child* | children |
| 7 | The contracted form of *will not* | won't |
| 8 | The contracted form of *I would* (or *I had*) | I'd |
| 9 | The contracted form of *cannot* | can't |
| 10 | The abbreviation *adj.* in a dictionary entry means | adjective |

**Rule to remember:** the class of a word depends on its job in the sentence, not on its spelling. *Work* is a verb in "I work" and a noun in "My work is done". The apostrophe in a contraction marks the missing letters.

## 17.2 Tenses

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | She ____ to school every day. (go) | goes |
| 2 | They ____ football when it started to rain. (play) | were playing |
| 3 | I ____ my homework already. (finish) | have finished |
| 4 | By 2030, he ____ his degree. (complete) | will have completed |
| 5 | We ____ in Pokhara since 2018. (live) | have lived / have been living |
| 6 | He ____ before I arrived. (leave) | had left |
| 7 | Water ____ at 100 degrees Celsius. (boil) | boils |
| 8 | Look! It ____. (rain) | is raining |
| 9 | Tomorrow at this time, I ____ to Doha. (fly) | will be flying |
| 10 | She ____ him yesterday. (not see) | did not see |

**Rule to remember:** *since* and *for* with a period up to now need the present perfect; *by* plus a future time needs the future perfect; a general truth takes the simple present.

## 17.3 Articles, determiners and quantifiers

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | ____ sun rises in the east. | The |
| 2 | She wants to be ____ engineer. | an |
| 3 | He is ____ honest boy. | an |
| 4 | ____ Himalayas are in the north of Nepal. | The |
| 5 | I have ____ few friends here, so I am not lonely. | a |
| 6 | There isn't ____ milk in the cup. | any |
| 7 | How ____ rupees do you have? | many |
| 8 | ____ student has a copy of the book. (each one separately) | Every / Each |
| 9 | ____ of my two brothers is married. (not one, not the other) | Neither |
| 10 | He plays ____ guitar very well. | the |

**Rule to remember:** *an* goes before a vowel *sound*, so *an honest* and *an hour* but *a university*. *A few* is positive (some); *few* alone is negative (almost none). *Many* goes with countable nouns and *much* with uncountable ones.

## 17.5 Passive voice and causatives

| No. | Active or prompt | Passive or answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | They built the temple in 1800. | The temple was built in 1800. |
| 2 | Someone is repairing the road. | The road is being repaired. |
| 3 | The teacher has praised Maya. | Maya has been praised by the teacher. |
| 4 | Who broke the window? | By whom was the window broken? |
| 5 | She will write a letter. | A letter will be written by her. |
| 6 | I ____ my hair cut yesterday. (have) | had |
| 7 | She ____ the house painted every year. (get) | gets |
| 8 | The boss made him work late. | He was made to work late. |
| 9 | People say that he is rich. | It is said that he is rich. |
| 10 | Open the door. | Let the door be opened. |

**Rule to remember:** the passive is *be* plus the past participle, and *be* carries the tense. *Have something done* means that someone else does the job for you.

## 17.6 Reported speech

| No. | Direct speech | Reported speech |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | He said, "I am hungry." | He said that he was hungry. |
| 2 | She said, "I have lost my pen." | She said that she had lost her pen. |
| 3 | "Where are you going?" he asked me. | He asked me where I was going. |
| 4 | "Do you like tea?" she asked. | She asked if I liked tea. |
| 5 | "Please sit down," the teacher said to us. | The teacher requested us to sit down. |
| 6 | "Don't touch the wire," mother said to me. | Mother told me not to touch the wire. |
| 7 | "I will call you tomorrow," he said. | He said that he would call me the next day. |
| 8 | "What a lovely day!" she said. | She exclaimed that it was a very lovely day. |
| 9 | "I can speak French," said Rita. | Rita said that she could speak French. |
| 10 | He said, "The earth moves round the sun." | He said that the earth moves round the sun. |

**Rule to remember:** a reporting verb in the past moves the tense one step back; *tomorrow* becomes *the next day* and *now* becomes *then*. A universal truth keeps its tense (item 10).

## 17.7 Conditional sentences

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | If you heat ice, it ____. (melt) | melts |
| 2 | If it rains, we ____ at home. (stay) | will stay |
| 3 | If I were a bird, I ____. (fly) | would fly |
| 4 | If she had worked hard, she ____ the exam. (pass) | would have passed |
| 5 | Unless you hurry, you ____ late. (be) | will be |
| 6 | I wish I ____ more time. (have) | had |
| 7 | If he ____ earlier, he would have caught the bus. (leave) | had left |
| 8 | ____ I known, I would have come. (inverted) | Had |
| 9 | If I ____ you, I would apologise. (be) | were |
| 10 | We shall go on a picnic provided it ____. (not rain) | does not rain |

**Rule to remember:** zero conditional is present with present; first is *if* plus present, *will*; second is *if* plus past, *would*; third is *if* plus past perfect, *would have*. *Unless* already means *if not*, so do not add another negative.

## 17.9 Question tags, questions and subject-verb agreement

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | You are a student, ____? | aren't you |
| 2 | She doesn't smoke, ____? | does she |
| 3 | Let's start, ____? | shall we |
| 4 | Open the window, ____? | will you |
| 5 | Everyone ____ here. (be) | is |
| 6 | Neither of the answers ____ correct. (be) | is |
| 7 | The teacher, with his students, ____ going on a trip. (be) | is |
| 8 | Ram or his brothers ____ coming. (be) | are |
| 9 | The police ____ arrived. (has or have) | have |
| 10 | "____ is your name?" (question word) | What |

**Rule to remember:** with *either...or* the verb agrees with the nearer noun (item 8); words between commas, such as *with his students*, do not change the verb (item 7). *Police* is always plural.

## 17.10 Prepositions, adjectives and adverbs

| No. | Item | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | She is afraid ____ spiders. | of |
| 2 | He is good ____ chess. | at |
| 3 | I will meet you ____ Monday. | on |
| 4 | She was born ____ 2007. | in |
| 5 | This is the ____ book I have read. (interesting) | most interesting |
| 6 | He drives very ____. (careful) | carefully |
| 7 | My brother is ____ than me. (tall) | taller |
| 8 | The soup tastes ____. (bad or badly) | bad |
| 9 | He worked ____ to pass the test. (hard or hardly) | hard |
| 10 | We walked ____ the bridge. (from one side to the other) | across |

**Rule to remember:** linking verbs such as *taste, look, feel, seem* take an adjective, not an adverb. *Hardly* means almost not; *hard* is the adverb that means with great effort.

## Chapter summary

- Q7 and Q8 together are **15 marks of one-mark items**; attempt every one.
- Tenses: *since* and *for* need the present perfect; *by* plus a future time needs the future perfect.
- Articles go by the *sound* of the next word; *a few* means some and *few* means almost none.
- Modals: *must* is obligation or certainty; *should* is advice; *needn't* means it is not necessary; *used to* is a past habit.
- Passive is *be* plus the past participle; the tense is carried by *be*.
- Reported speech moves the tense one step back and changes *tomorrow* to *the next day*; universal truths do not change.
- Conditionals: *if* + present gives *will*; *if* + past gives *would*; *if* + past perfect gives *would have*.
- For tags, reverse the polarity; for agreement, find the real subject and ignore the words between commas.

## Practice questions

**Question 7: Grammar (1 mark each)**

1. Fill in the blank with the correct form: She ____ (teach) English for ten years now. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
2. Fill in the blank: ____ you like some tea? (offer) <span class="marks">[1]</span>
3. Change into the passive voice: The students are cleaning the classroom. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
4. Report: "I don't know the answer," said Sunil. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
5. Add the question tag: Nobody called, ____? <span class="marks">[1]</span>

::: note Answers to Question 7
**1.** has been teaching (or has taught) — a period up to now with *for ten years*.
**2.** Would — *Would you like* is the polite form of an offer.
**3.** The classroom is being cleaned by the students.
**4.** Sunil said that he did not know the answer.
**5.** did they — *nobody* makes the clause negative, so the tag is positive.
:::

**Question 8: Vocabulary (1 mark each)**

1. Write the opposite of the word *regular* by using a prefix. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
2. Give a synonym of *ancient*. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
3. What does the idiom *spill the beans* mean? <span class="marks">[1]</span>

::: note Answers to Question 8
**1.** irregular.
**2.** very old (or antique).
**3.** to reveal a secret by mistake.

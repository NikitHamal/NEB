These questions practise respectful, factual health learning. They do not replace professional diagnosis or emergency support.

1. Name the brain, spinal cord and nerves as parts of which body system? <span class="marks">[1]</span>
2. What are hormones? <span class="marks">[1]</span>
3. State one difference between nervous and endocrine communication. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
4. A classmate is worried about a normal body change during puberty. Write two respectful actions you could take. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
5. Put these in a correct signal sequence: muscle response, receptor, sensory nerve, brain/spinal cord, motor nerve. <span class="marks">[3]</span>
6. Explain why online rumours are not a safe source for a persistent health concern. <span class="marks">[2]</span>

::: note Answer key
**1.** Nervous system.  
**2.** Chemical messengers released by glands and carried in blood.  
**3.** Nerves carry fast signals; hormones travel through blood and often act more gradually.  
**4.** Listen without teasing, respect privacy, and suggest a trusted adult or health worker; any two suitable actions.  
**5.** Receptor, sensory nerve, brain/spinal cord, motor nerve, muscle response.  
**6.** Rumours may be inaccurate and cannot assess an individual; trusted adult or qualified health support is safer.
:::

These original questions practise sets and Venn diagrams. Show the region calculations, not only the final number.

1. Write the empty set using two accepted symbols. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
2. Let $A=\{1,3,5,7\}$ and $B=\{3,7\}$. State whether $B\subseteq A$. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
3. Give one pair of disjoint sets from daily life. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
4. In a group of 35 learners, 20 play chess, 14 play ludo and 6 play both. Find the number who play only chess. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
5. For question 4, find the number who play only ludo and the number who play neither game. <span class="marks">[3]</span>
6. A survey says 17 students prefer tea, 15 prefer milk and 9 prefer both. Draw a labelled two-set Venn diagram and find how many prefer at least one. <span class="marks">[4]</span>

::: note Answer key
**1.** $\emptyset$ and $\{\}$.  
**2.** Yes.  
**3.** Any valid example with no common member, such as even and odd numbers.  
**4.** $20-6=14$.  
**5.** Only ludo $=14$; neither $=35-(14+6+8)=7$.  
**6.** Only tea $=8$, only milk $=6$, both $=9$; at least one $=23$.
:::

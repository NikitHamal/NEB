Use these original questions after studying English Unit 1. They practise timetable reading, polite information requests, present-simple accuracy and purposeful writing.

## A. Quick retrieval

1. In a timetable, why should you read the action as well as the time? <span class="marks">[1]</span>
2. Choose the polite request:  
   a. Give me the form.  
   b. Could you please give me the form?  
   c. Form now. <span class="marks">[1]</span>
3. Complete: “Does the bus ___ at 8:30?” (leave/leaves) <span class="marks">[1]</span>
4. Write the stressed syllable in *information*. <span class="marks">[1]</span>

## B. Read and reason

| Time | Action |
|---|---|
| 08:30 | Meet at school gate |
| 08:45 | Board the bus |
| 09:30 | Arrive at museum |
| 13:15 | Begin return journey |
| 14:00 | Reach school |

5. How many minutes are available between meeting and boarding? <span class="marks">[1]</span>
6. Which action happens immediately before arrival at the museum? <span class="marks">[1]</span>
7. Write one polite question to ask about the museum-entry fee. <span class="marks">[2]</span>

## C. Write with purpose

8. Write a 60–80 word request to your class teacher for permission to join the museum visit. Include a greeting, purpose, one specific question and a suitable closing. <span class="marks">[5]</span>

::: note Answer key
**1.** Time tells when; action tells what to do.  
**2.** b.  
**3.** leave.  
**4.** ma.  
**5.** 15 minutes.  
**6.** Board the bus.  
**7.** Any complete, polite question is correct.  
**8.** Check the four requested parts, clarity and correct schedule language.
:::

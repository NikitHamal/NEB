These questions practise Unit 1 reasoning. Support a claim with a local example or a clearly stated connection.

1. Define development in a sentence that includes people’s wellbeing. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
2. Classify each as infrastructure or human resource: bridge, nurse, irrigation canal, trained electrician. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
3. Explain why a road project needs maintenance planning after construction. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
4. Give two questions a citizen can ask to judge whether a local project is inclusive. <span class="marks">[2]</span>
5. Prepare a short report plan for a nearby project. Include project name, purpose, users, one benefit, one challenge and information source. <span class="marks">[4]</span>

::: note Answer key
**1.** A good definition links development with improved opportunities, services or quality of life.  
**2.** Infrastructure: bridge, irrigation canal. Human resource: nurse, trained electrician.  
**3.** Maintenance keeps a project safe and useful and protects public investment.  
**4.** Examples: Who can use it safely? Is it accessible to people with disabilities?  
**5.** All six requested items should be specific and evidence-aware.
:::

What changed since v2

Icons

Hand drawn strokes.
Material Symbols Outlined everywhere, the same font the site and the app use. Filled only for verified, pinned and selected states.

Message

Follow and Message.
There is no messaging on the platform, so it is gone. Teachers and schools who add a number get a Call button in the header and a Contact card in Overview. Nobody else sees a call option.

Resources

Concept cards of our own invention.
The exact site card: art, type pill, subject icon, subject chip, title, grade and size, author, time and views. Rail size in Overview, two columns in the tab.

Posts

Heart to like.
The site post card with thumb_up, comments, views for the owner and Share. Pinned post keeps the new pin label and a primary border.

Open to

A row of coloured pills that cut off at the edge.
Four tiles, each with an icon shape, the offer and the specific (subject, time, place). Visitors tap to reach out, owners tap to edit. Every type has its own offers.

Studying now

A line of text.
Subject art, chapter, one segment per chapter, today's time, streak, the week as bars, and the resource being read.

Overview

A flat stack of lists.
It opens with three glance tiles, then the modules that matter for that profile. Every module has See all that opens its tab, and nothing lives only in Overview.

Art

2D pixel pictures.
Covers, thumbnails, books, collections and achievements come from the new M3X art library of 725 pieces. Open the library.

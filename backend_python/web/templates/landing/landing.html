Questions people ask first.

Is it really free?

Yes. Reading, downloading, the forum and the study books are free. Some creators sell their own courses in the marketplace, and those are clearly marked with a price.

Which classes and fields does it cover?

All of them. Every class, field and level is welcome, from school to Master's. Class 8, 10, 11 and 12 have the most material today, and the library grows with every upload.

Who writes the material?

Students and teachers upload most of the library, and moderators check it before it goes public. The NEBians study books are written by our team against the CDC syllabus.

Do I need an account?

Not to read. You need one to post in the forum, upload, bookmark and keep your place across devices.

Nepal's Open Learning Community & Educational Social Network

NEBians is Nepal's universal learning platform and educational social network. While we provide comprehensive study materials including NEB past papers, model questions, and syllabi, NEBians is not limited to NEB or Classes 11 and 12. We are a community built for everyone — school and college students, teachers, parents, educational institutions, and curious explorers across all subjects, disciplines, and learning stages in Nepal.

Frequently asked questions

Where can I find NEB past papers?

Browse the NEB past papers library filtered by subject and grade, or open a subject page for Class 11, Class 12 or SEE to see papers, notes and solutions together.

Where are NEB model questions?

All model sets live in the NEB model questions collection, grouped with notes, solutions and discussions per subject.

Is NEBians free for online learning in Nepal?

Yes. The library, videos, forum and AI study tools are free, making NEBians one of the most accessible eLearning options in Nepal for Nepal online study.

Frequently Asked Questions About NEBians & Learning in Nepal

Is NEBians only for NEB (Class 11 & 12) students?

No. While the name originated from the high demand for NEB exam resources, NEBians is an open learning community and social platform for all of Nepal. It serves school students (SEE), higher secondary students, university learners, teachers, parents, educational institutions, and independent explorers.

Who can join and use NEBians?

Anyone interested in learning or teaching! Teachers can share lecture notes and guide students; institutions can engage with learners; and curious minds can explore science, technology, mathematics, and literature.

Are resources on NEBians free to access?

Yes, public notes, past question papers, and discussions are completely free to view and download with our built-in PDF viewer and tools.
